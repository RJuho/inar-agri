## Short answer

This dive is an iteration of <a href={`${base}deep-dive/disturbance-size/`}>Deep Dive #1, *Size of management disturbances*</a>.
It uses the same windows and rules. It adds Anttila and Särkisuo to the sites. It asks two new questions: how do cleaning
cuts, silage harvests and grain harvests differ? On which day does the flux drop? A higher NEE means less net uptake of CO₂.
The question about fertilisation is now in <a href={`${base}deep-dive/fertilisation-weather/`}>Deep Dive #5, *Fertilisation and GPP: more change than the weather explains?*</a>.

## Data and method

**Events.** The events come from `db/events.parquet`: <Num k="counts.events" digits={0} /> events of the five sites, of these
<Num k="counts.harvestCutEvents" digits={0} /> harvests or cuts. The events of Haltiala, Qvidja and Viikki come from their management
files <Cite ids={['haltiala-management', 'qvidja-management', 'viikki-management']} />. The harvest and cut events of
Anttila and Särkisuo of 2021 and 2022 come from Shurpali et al.: Table 2 and the text, PDF p. 2 and p. 3; the annual yields come from Table 4
<Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 2 and p. 3, Table 2; yields in Table 4, PDF p. 8" />. The events of 2023 and the yields per cut
of these two sites come from the project file `data/Agri_site_management_events 2018-2025.xlsx`. This file also gives some
dates, amounts and new events of Qvidja and Viikki. Maktau is not in this dive.

**Harvest groups.** We put each harvest or cut episode in one of three groups:

- *silage harvest*: a harvest that removes the grass (silage, hay, whole crop),
- *cleaning cut*: a cut where the grass stays on the field (Qvidja "puhdistusniitto"),
- *grain or bean harvest*: a harvest whose text names threshing, grain, faba bean, oats or barley, and not a cut, silage or grass.

Harvest and cut events of a site within <Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days are one episode. Fertilisation, tillage and
grazing do not join a harvest episode. <Num k="counts.overrides" digits={0} /> episodes have a manual correction of the dates
(column "Correction" of the episode table): for example, the Haltiala harvest of 2021 started on the date in the text of the
file, and at Qvidja in 2023 a cleaning cut and a silage harvest on the next day are one silage episode. This gives
<Num k="counts.episodes" digits={0} /> episodes: <Num k="counts.perGroup.silageHarvest" digits={0} /> silage harvests,
<Num k="counts.perGroup.cleaningCut" digits={0} /> cleaning cuts and <Num k="counts.perGroup.grainHarvest" digits={0} /> grain or bean harvests.

**Daily values.** NEE, GPP and Reco are the daily sums of the gap-filled, partitioned fluxes in {U} from
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. The measured day-time NEE comes from
`data/calculated/fluxes_30min.parquet` (no gap filling).

**Windows and rules.** These are the same as in Deep Dive #1, so that the dives are comparable. The *before* window is the
<Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} /> days before the first day of the episode. The *after* window is the
<Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days after the last day. The *change* is the mean after minus the mean before. A check
uses <Num k="method.shortDays" digits={0} />-day windows. The flux result uses an episode only when it passes three rules:

- **Rule 1.** No grazing, tillage or other harvest or cut episode touches a window.
- **Rule 2.** Each window has at least <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with NEE, GPP and Reco.
- **Rule 3.** The mean measured fraction of NEE is at least <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % in each window.

<Num k="counts.fluxUsed" digits={0} /> episodes passed (<Num k="counts.fluxExclusions.overlap" digits={0} /> failed rule 1,
<Num k="counts.fluxExclusions.too-few-days" digits={0} /> rule 2, <Num k="counts.fluxExclusions.low-measured-fraction" digits={0} /> rule 3).
A fertilisation in a window does not exclude a harvest episode. We give each group also without these episodes. The
*relative change of GPP* is the change in percent of the mean GPP before (at least <Num k="method.minGppBefore" digits={1} unit={U} />).

**Recovery.** The *recovery share* is the mean GPP of days <Num k="method.recoveryFrom" digits={0} /> to
<Num k="method.recoveryTo" digits={0} /> after the last day, in percent of the mean before. It is empty when another harvest,
cut or grazing starts in the first <Num k="method.recoveryTo" digits={0} /> days. Tillage does not block the recovery. The *days to recover* are the first day after
the episode when the centred <Num k="method.recoveryRunDays" digits={0} />-day running mean of GPP reaches
<Num k="method.recoverySharePct" digits={0} /> % of the mean before. We searched to day <Num k="method.recoveryMaxDays" digits={0} />.
An episode is *censored* when the next harvest, cut or grazing starts first, when the data end, or when GPP does not reach
the level in <Num k="method.recoveryMaxDays" digits={0} /> days. We used <Num k="method.recoverySharePct" digits={0} /> % and not
the full level, because GPP in late summer often does not return to its level of June for seasonal reasons.

**Yield and canopy.** We fitted the change of GPP against the dry-matter yield of the same harvest (least squares, Pearson r).
Qvidja, Anttila and Särkisuo give a yield per harvest. Viikki gives the yield of the year on the last harvest of the year;
we did not use it in the fit. We also fitted the relative change
of GPP against the mean GPP before, a simple index of the canopy, for all used silage harvests.

**Timing.** For each episode we searched the day of the largest drop of daily GPP. A candidate day *t* goes from
<Num k="method.searchFrom" sign digits={0} /> days before the first day to <Num k="method.searchTo" sign digits={0} /> days after the
last day. The drop at *t* is the mean of the <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> days before *t* minus the mean of *t* and the
following days (<Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> days). Each side needs <Num k="method.minSideGpp" digits={0} /> days with a value.
The largest drop is *clear* when the mean before is at least <Num k="method.minBeforeGpp" digits={0} unit={U} /> and the drop is at
least <Num k="method.minDropPct" digits={0} /> % of it. The *offset* is the detected day minus the first recorded date in the file. For an episode with a corrected start (for example Haltiala 2021), the offset still counts from the first date in the file, not from the corrected start.
A negative offset means that the flux dropped before the recorded date. As a check, we did the same search on the daily mean
of the *measured* day-time NEE (no gap filling, at least <Num k="method.minMeasuredHalfHours" digits={0} /> half hours per day,
<Num k="method.minSideNee" digits={0} /> days per side, search on −NEE). The two offsets *agree* when they differ by not more than
<Num k="method.agreeDays" digits={0} /> day. The *pre-drop* is the change of the mean GPP of days <Num k="method.preNearFrom" sign digits={0} />
to <Num k="method.preNearTo" sign digits={0} /> against days <Num k="method.preFarFrom" sign digits={0} /> to
<Num k="method.preFarTo" sign digits={0} />, in percent. An episode is not used for the timing when grazing, tillage or another
harvest episode is near: <Num k="counts.timingUsed" digits={0} /> episodes are used.

**Statistics.** Per group and per site: n, mean, SD and a <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % percentile
bootstrap interval of the mean (<Num k="method.bootstrapResamples" digits={0} /> resamples, fixed seed). An interval needs at
least three episodes. With fewer than about ten episodes it is not reliable.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Small n.** There are <Num k="groups.cleaningCut.nFlux" digits={0} /> used cleaning cuts (all at Qvidja) and
  <Num k="groups.grainHarvest.nFlux" digits={0} /> grain or bean harvests. Their intervals are not reliable; the tables give the
  single episodes. Per site, the silage group has <Num k="groups.silageHarvest.bySite.anttila.nFlux" digits={0} /> episodes or fewer
  except at Qvidja. The SD of the change of NEE after a silage harvest is <Num k="groups.silageHarvest.nee.delta.sd" unit={U} />
  (n = <Num k="groups.silageHarvest.nFlux" digits={0} />).
- **Gap filling around harvests.** GPP and Reco are model values from the gap-filled NEE <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. Some
  episodes have days with a measured fraction below <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % in the search span (column
  "Low-frac days"). In Haltiala 2021 the days around the harvest have almost no measured values. A gap-filled drop can be
  smoother or earlier than the real drop. The measured-NEE offset is the check, but it is available for few episodes at Viikki and for none at Haltiala.
- **Negative daily GPP.** GPP is Reco − NEE and is not limited to positive values <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. Days with a
  negative GPP occur after the grain harvests (Figure 4) and make the percent values of low-GPP episodes unstable.
- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as local time. These
  values are <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours early (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>). On a daily scale
  this moves <Num k="method.utcShiftHalfHours" digits={0} /> of the <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours of a day to the
  next day. A daily change-point can move by at most one day. A shift of <Num k="method.utcShiftHalfHours" digits={0} /> half hours
  changes a <Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} />-day mean only through its first and its last day.
- **Event dates.** The Qvidja management file gives grazing in June 2021 and June 2022 <Cite ids={['qvidja-management']} />. The project
  file `data/Agri_site_management_events 2018-2025.xlsx` gives August 2021 and August 2022, and Heimsch et al. 2024 also describe grazing in
  August 2021 and August 2022 <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="PDF p. 2" />. We used the dates of the project file. The project file
  also moved some Qvidja harvest dates by one to twelve days. The Viikki file
  and Gao et al. give the harvests of 2022 in July and August <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="PDF p. 3 and Table A2, PDF p. 13" />.
  The Anttila and Särkisuo dates of 2021 and 2022 come from one article: Table 2 and the text, PDF p. 2 and p. 3; the annual
  yields come from Table 4 <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 2 and p. 3, Table 2; yields in Table 4, PDF p. 8" />. The dates of
  2023 and the yields per cut come from the project file. For the second cut of 2022 at Anttila, the project file gives
  8 August and the article 8 September; we used the project file.
- **Unrecorded events.** Anttila and Särkisuo have no own management file. Their events come from one article (2021 and 2022)
  and from the project file (2022 and 2023). Only events with an exact day are rows. The Särkisuo whole-crop harvest of July 2021 has no day in the article and is not an event <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 2" />. Särkisuo has no flux data in 2021. Events not in the two sources can be in the windows of all sites.
- **Search window.** The Haltiala 2022 drop is at the edge of the search span. The decrease of GPP over the weeks before is
  consistent with the senescence of the oats, not with the harvest day.
- **Association.** The changes are *associated with* the events. The weather, the season and the crop stage also change in
  the same windows.

## Reproduce

The numbers on this page come from `site/src/deep-dives/disturbance-detail.json`. The script
[`scripts/deep-dives/disturbance-detail.ts`](https://github.com/RJuho/inar-agri/blob/main/scripts/deep-dives/disturbance-detail.ts)
writes this file. It reads only `db/events.parquet`, `db/sites.parquet`,
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` and `data/calculated/fluxes_30min.parquet`. The pure helpers are in
`scripts/lib/deep-dive/harvest-groups.ts`, `change-point.ts`, `disturbance.ts` and `stats.ts`
(unit tests `*.test.ts`).

```sh
npm run build:db && npm run calc           # make the inputs
npm run deep-dives -- disturbance-detail   # write the JSON again
npm run deep-dives -- --check              # compare a fresh run with the committed JSON
```
