## Short answer

We found <Num k="totals.episodes" digits={0} /> episodes of natural events at the six sites. For each class and site,
we compared the daily NEE on the event days with the daily NEE on the other used days of the same calendar months.
An NEE below zero is a net uptake of CO₂. A positive difference means less uptake or more release on the event days.

- **Dry spells** were associated with more net uptake at the four Finnish sites with rain data, for example Qvidja
  <Num k="classes.drySpell.qvidja.diff" sign unit={U} /> (n = <Num k="classes.drySpell.qvidja.event.n" digits={0} /> days).
- **Heavy rain** days were associated with less uptake at all five sites with rain data, but the number of days is
  small (for example Qvidja n = <Num k="classes.heavyRain.qvidja.event.n" digits={0} />,
  difference <Num k="classes.heavyRain.qvidja.diff" sign unit={U} />).
- **Soil drought** gave a mixed result. It was associated with less uptake at Qvidja
  (<Num k="classes.soilDrought.qvidja.diff" sign unit={U} />, n = <Num k="classes.soilDrought.qvidja.event.n" digits={0} />)
  and with more uptake at Haltiala
  (<Num k="classes.soilDrought.haltiala.diff" sign unit={U} />, n = <Num k="classes.soilDrought.haltiala.event.n" digits={0} />)
  and Viikki (<Num k="classes.soilDrought.viikki.diff" sign unit={U} />, n = <Num k="classes.soilDrought.viikki.event.n" digits={0} />).
- **Heat** days were associated with more uptake at Haltiala (<Num k="classes.heat.haltiala.diff" sign unit={U} />,
  n = <Num k="classes.heat.haltiala.event.n" digits={0} />) and Viikki (<Num k="classes.heat.viikki.diff" sign unit={U} />,
  n = <Num k="classes.heat.viikki.event.n" digits={0} />). They were associated with less uptake at the other four sites
  (see the class table).
- **Cold spells** and the **zero curtain** (frozen soil) are winter classes. On these days NEE was a small release.
  At most sites it was a little smaller than on the other days of the same months, for example cold spells at Qvidja
  <Num k="classes.coldSpell.qvidja.event.mean" unit={U} /> (SD <Num k="classes.coldSpell.qvidja.event.sd" />,
  n = <Num k="classes.coldSpell.qvidja.event.n" digits={0} />) against <Num k="classes.coldSpell.qvidja.reference.mean" unit={U} />
  (SD <Num k="classes.coldSpell.qvidja.reference.sd" />, n = <Num k="classes.coldSpell.qvidja.reference.n" digits={0} />).
- Daily NEE decreased with the air temperature at the five Finnish sites. Pearson r was from
  <Num k="anscombe.ta.viikki.r" /> at Viikki (n = <Num k="anscombe.ta.viikki.n" digits={0} /> days) to
  <Num k="anscombe.ta.qvidja.r" /> at Qvidja (n = <Num k="anscombe.ta.qvidja.n" digits={0} />). At Maktau daily NEE
  increased a little with it (r = <Num k="anscombe.ta.maktau.r" />, n = <Num k="anscombe.ta.maktau.n" digits={0} />).

## Data and method

**Days.** A local day starts at midnight site local time. This is `ts_start` plus the UTC offset of the site. A daily
value of a variable needs <Num k="method.minHalfHours" digits={0} /> or more of the <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} />
half hours. Before the daily means, we removed half hours with an air temperature below
<Num k="method.taSpikeLimit" digits={0} unit="°C" />. This removes one logger row at Särkisuo
(<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-03`}>finding SAR-03</a>). The daily NEE is `nee_f_gc` (g C m⁻² d⁻¹) of
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. At the five Finnish sites it is the gap-filled NEE of
the data provider. Only where the provider column is empty (some rows of Viikki in 2023), the gap filling (MDS) of this
project fills it. At Maktau the daily NEE is the MDS gap filling of this project. At Maktau, all classes, the soil
percentiles and the NEE use only the years <Num k="method.maktauFluxYears.from" digits={0} /> to
<Num k="method.maktauFluxYears.to" digits={0} />.

**Human disturbances.** We removed the days from <Num k="method.humanWindow.before" digits={0} /> days before to
<Num k="method.humanWindow.after" digits={0} /> days after each dated management event of `db/events.parquet`
(harvest, cut, grazing, fertilisation, tillage, sowing, spraying and other). The other days are the *used days*.
This removed <Num k="sites.qvidja.days.human" digits={0} /> days at Qvidja, <Num k="sites.viikki.days.human" digits={0} />
at Viikki and <Num k="sites.haltiala.days.human" digits={0} /> at Haltiala. The mask uses the events of these three
sites only. The events of Anttila, Särkisuo and Maktau from the articles were added to the database after this dive
was computed, and they are not used, thus all days of these sites are used. The weather classes are detected on all days, because the weather does not
depend on the management. The removed days are not used in the NEE analysis and in the soil classes. In total there are
<Num k="totals.usedDays" digits={0} /> used days, <Num k="totals.usedNeeDays" digits={0} /> of them with NEE.

**Classes.** Weather classes use absolute limits. Soil classes use the percentiles of each site.

- *Frost*: daily minimum air temperature below <Num k="method.frost.belowC" digits={0} unit="°C" />, only from May to
  September, Finnish sites only.
- *Heat*: daily maximum air temperature above <Num k="method.heat.finlandC" digits={0} unit="°C" /> (Finland) or above
  <Num k="method.heat.maktauC" digits={0} unit="°C" /> (Maktau). A run of <Num k="method.heat.waveDays" digits={0} /> or
  more heat days is marked as a heat wave in the episode table.
- *Cold spell*: daily mean air temperature below <Num k="method.coldSpellC" digits={0} unit="°C" /> (Finland).
- *Heavy rain*: more than <Num k="method.heavyRainMm" digits={0} unit="mm" /> rain in a day. Haltiala has no rain
  sensor, thus it has no heavy rain and no dry spells.
- *Dry spell*: <Num k="method.drySpell.days" digits={0} /> or more days in a row with less than
  <Num k="method.drySpell.mmPerDay" digits={0} unit="mm" /> rain, from May to September (Finland). A day without a rain
  value stops the run.
- *Failed rains* (Maktau): a rain season (March to May or September to November) with a rain sum below the median of
  the seasonal sums of the years <Num k="method.maktauFluxYears.from" digits={0} /> to
  <Num k="method.maktauFluxYears.to" digits={0} /> (the season itself included). The mean soil water content of the
  season must also be below the median of the season means of the same years, the season itself included
  (quantile <Num k="method.failedRains.swcQuantile" digits={1} />). A season needs
  <Num k="method.failedRains.minSeasonDays" digits={0} /> or more days with rain data. All days of a failed season
  are event days.
- *Soil drought* and *soil wet*: daily mean soil water content below the
  <Num k="method.soil.p10" percent digits={0} unit="%" /> or above the <Num k="method.soil.p90" percent digits={0} unit="%" />
  percentile of the used days of the site. Because both tails have the same size, the two classes have the same number
  of days at a site, except for ties of equal values. At Qvidja, Viikki and Haltiala, we used the soil water only on days
  with a daily mean soil temperature above <Num k="method.soil.tsoilMinC" digits={1} unit="°C" />, because the sensors
  read a too low value in frozen soil.
- *Zero curtain*: daily mean soil temperature within ±<Num k="method.zeroCurtain.bandC" digits={1} unit="°C" /> of zero
  for <Num k="method.zeroCurtain.days" digits={0} /> or more days in a row (Finland). We use it as an indirect sign of
  frozen or snow-covered soil, because the database has no snow depth.

Consecutive days of a class make one *episode*. A day can be in more than one class.

**Statistics.** For each class and site with <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> or more used event days with NEE, we
calculated n, mean and SD of the daily NEE on the event days and on the *reference days*. The reference days are the
non-event used days in the calendar months that have at least one used event day with NEE at that site. The difference
is the event mean minus the reference mean. For the Anscombe panels of the two scatter figures we calculated, per site and over all used days with NEE, n, the means, the variances, Pearson r, R² and the
least-squares line of daily NEE against the daily mean air temperature and against the percentile rank of the daily
soil water content within the site. The figures draw a sample of up to
<Num k="method.pointsPerSite" digits={0} /> days per site. The periods are spring (March to May), summer (June to
August), autumn (September to November) and winter (December to February). A winter has the year of its January.
For each period and year we added the used days over all sites.

<Num k="classes.heavyRain.viikki.diff" sign unit={U} /> (n = <Num k="classes.heavyRain.viikki.event.n" digits={0} />,

and the short rains (October to December) of the region, with more frequent droughts in recent years
<Cite ids={['rasanen2026']} note="p. 2" />, and name 2021 and 2022 as dry years <Cite ids={['rasanen2026']} note="p. 9" />.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Association, not cause.** The event days differ from the reference days in more than one way (light, crop stage,
  season inside the month). The differences are *associated with* the events. The SD of the daily NEE is often larger
  than the difference, for example heat at Qvidja: SD <Num k="classes.heat.qvidja.event.sd" /> on
  <Num k="classes.heat.qvidja.event.n" digits={0} /> event days and <Num k="classes.heat.qvidja.reference.sd" /> on
  <Num k="classes.heat.qvidja.reference.n" digits={0} /> reference days.
- **Small n.** Heavy rain at Anttila and Särkisuo (n = <Num k="classes.heavyRain.anttila.event.n" digits={0} /> and
  <Num k="classes.heavyRain.sarkisuo.event.n" digits={0} />) and frost at Viikki (n = <Num k="classes.frost.viikki.event.n" digits={0} />)
  have few days. Their results are not reliable.
- **Human-disturbance window.** The weather classes are detected on all days. The removed days (<Num k="method.humanWindow.before" digits={0} />
  days before to <Num k="method.humanWindow.after" digits={0} /> days after each event) apply to the NEE and soil
  analysis only. The mask uses the events of Haltiala, Qvidja and Viikki. The events of Anttila, Särkisuo and Maktau
  from the articles were added later and are not used, thus their NEE can include management effects (for example the
  two cuts of 2022 at Anttila and Särkisuo).
- **Soil drought in winter.** At Anttila and Särkisuo the soil water is used also in frozen soil, thus part of their
  soil drought days are winter days with a low sensor reading, not dry soil.
- **Zero curtain is an indirect sign.** The database has no snow depth and no water table. A flat soil temperature near zero
  is also found in sensor data (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-04`}>finding SAR-04</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-05`}>ANT-05</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-03`}>VII-03</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-HAL-04`}>HAL-04</a>). The class is very common (for example
  <Num k="counts.sarkisuo.zeroCurtain.days" digits={0} /> days at Särkisuo) and is a winter state, not a short event.
- **Soil water.** We used site percentiles, not absolute values, because the sensors and soils differ. The Qvidja value
  is a conversion from sensor volts (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-01`}>finding QVI-01</a>) with a sensor
  upper limit in wet periods (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-02`}>QVI-02</a>) and low values in frozen soil
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-03`}>QVI-03</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-16`}>QVI-16</a>).
  Haltiala has a coarse resolution with long flat periods
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-HAL-01`}>finding HAL-01</a>) and a higher level in 2021
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-HAL-03`}>HAL-03</a>), which moves the 2021 days to the wet tail.
  Heimsch et al. also link winter drops of the soil moisture at Qvidja to soil freezing <Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="p. 3472" />.
- **Rain.** Haltiala has no rain data. The Qvidja rain gauge has a gap from December 2021 to May 2022
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-08`}>finding QVI-08</a>); a missing day stops a dry spell.
- **Maktau.** The logger stored day-time rows only from July 2023 to January 2024
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-01`}>finding MKT-01</a>), thus these days fail the
  <Num k="method.minHalfHours" digits={0} />-half-hour rule, and the seasons {untested.join(' and ')} have no failed-rains
  result. VPD fill values affect the MDS gap filling (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-02`}>MKT-02</a>), and the
  day-time air temperature of 2024 is higher than in the other years (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-08`}>MKT-08</a>),
  which can add heat days. The MDS gap filling of Maktau has gaps of several months in two years. The documentation
  calls the result of these years not reliable <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. Our seasons use full calendar months; the short rains are from October to December
  <Cite ids={['rasanen2026']} note="p. 2" />, thus September to November covers them only in part.
- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as local time,
  so the values are <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours early
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>findings ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>). On a daily scale this moves
  <Num k="method.utcShiftHalfHours" digits={0} /> of the <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours of a day to
  the next day. For daily minimum, maximum and mean values this effect is small.
- **Sample in the figures.** The points of the two scatter figures are a repeatable random sample of up to
  <Num k="method.pointsPerSite" digits={0} /> days per site. The statistics in the boxes and the tables use all used days.
- **Period-years added over all sites** mix different sets of sites: for example <Num k="periods.years.1.sitesWithData" digits={0} /> site
  in summer 2018 and <Num k="periods.years.16.sitesWithData" digits={0} /> sites in summer 2022. The last winter
  (2025/26) has December only. Use the per-site means of the table to compare years.

## Reproduce

The numbers on this page come from `site/src/deep-dives/natural-events.json`. The script
[`scripts/deep-dives/natural-events.ts`](https://github.com/RJuho/inar-agri/blob/main/scripts/deep-dives/natural-events.ts)
writes this file. It reads only `db/sites.parquet`, `db/observations.parquet`, `db/events.parquet` and
`data/calculated/daily.parquet`. The pure helpers are in `scripts/lib/deep-dive/natural-events.ts` (unit test
`natural-events.test.ts`).

```sh
npm run build:db && npm run calc         # make the inputs
npm run deep-dives -- natural-events     # write the JSON again
npm run deep-dives -- --check            # compare a fresh run with the committed JSON
```
