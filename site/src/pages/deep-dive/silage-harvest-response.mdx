## Short answer

This dive follows the daily GPP, TER and NEE of four grasslands around a silage harvest. TER is the total ecosystem
respiration (the column `reco` of the night-time partitioning). NEE is the gap-filled NEE; NEE below zero is uptake. The
window runs from <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before to <Num k="method.compositeTo" digits={0} /> days after
the harvest. Fluxes are in {U}. The summaries use the <Num k="counts.used" digits={0} /> used episodes of
<Num k="counts.silageEpisodes" digits={0} />.

- **Drop and rise.** In the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days after a harvest, the mean daily GPP was
  <Num k="summary.all.deltaGpp.mean" unit={U} /> lower than in the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before
  (n = <Num k="summary.all.n" digits={0} />, SD <Num k="summary.all.deltaGpp.sd" />, <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> %
  interval <Num k="summary.all.deltaGpp.ciLo" /> to <Num k="summary.all.deltaGpp.ciHi" />). That is
  <Num k="summary.all.relGpp.mean" sign digits={0} unit="%" /> of the GPP before (interval
  <Num k="summary.all.relGpp.ciLo" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="summary.all.relGpp.ciHi" sign digits={0} /> %). NEE rose by
  <Num k="summary.all.deltaNee.mean" sign unit={U} /> (SD <Num k="summary.all.deltaNee.sd" />, interval
  <Num k="summary.all.deltaNee.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="summary.all.deltaNee.ciHi" sign />), or
  <Num k="summary.all.relNee.mean" sign digits={0} unit="%" /> of the GPP before (interval
  <Num k="summary.all.relNee.ciLo" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="summary.all.relNee.ciHi" sign digits={0} /> %). TER changed by
  <Num k="summary.all.deltaReco.mean" sign unit={U} /> (SD <Num k="summary.all.deltaReco.sd" />, interval
  <Num k="summary.all.deltaReco.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="summary.all.deltaReco.ciHi" sign />), or
  <Num k="summary.all.relReco.mean" sign digits={0} unit="%" /> of the GPP before (interval
  <Num k="summary.all.relReco.ciLo" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="summary.all.relReco.ciHi" sign digits={0} /> %); this interval
  includes zero.
- **Recovery.** In the days <Num k="method.lateDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.lateDays.1" sign digits={0} /> after
  the harvest, GPP was <Num k="summary.all.recoveryShare.mean" digits={0} unit="%" /> of its level before
  (n = <Num k="summary.all.recoveryShare.n" digits={0} />, SD <Num k="summary.all.recoveryShare.sd" digits={0} />, interval
  <Num k="summary.all.recoveryShare.ciLo" digits={0} /> to <Num k="summary.all.recoveryShare.ciHi" digits={0} /> %).
  <Num k="summary.all.nRecovered" digits={0} /> of <Num k="summary.all.n" digits={0} /> episodes came back to
  <Num k="method.recoverySharePct" digits={0} /> % of the GPP before within <Num k="method.recoveryMaxDays" digits={0} /> days;
  among these, the mean time was <Num k="summary.all.daysToRecover.mean" digits={1} unit="days" /> (median
  <Num k="summary.all.daysToRecover.median" digits={1} />, SD <Num k="summary.all.daysToRecover.sd" digits={1} />, interval
  <Num k="summary.all.daysToRecover.ciLo" digits={1} /> to <Num k="summary.all.daysToRecover.ciHi" digits={1} /> days). The
  other <Num k="summary.all.nCensored" digits={0} /> episodes did not reach this level, so the mean does not include them.
- **Timing.** <Num k="summary.all.clearDrops" digits={0} /> of <Num k="summary.all.timingUsed" digits={0} /> used episodes
  have a clear GPP drop. The detected day minus the recorded date was <Num k="summary.all.gppOffset.mean" sign digits={1} unit="days" />
  on average, that is, before the recorded date (SD <Num k="summary.all.gppOffset.sd" digits={1} />, interval
  <Num k="summary.all.gppOffset.ciLo" sign digits={1} /> to <Num k="summary.all.gppOffset.ciHi" sign digits={1} /> days, median

The changes are associated with the harvests. The weather, the season and the crop stage change in the same windows. The
analysis does not show that a harvest caused a change.

## Data and method

**Snapshot and script.** The script `analysis/silage-harvest-response/silage-harvest-response.py` reads the harmonised
database snapshot built on {d.dataVersion.snapshot.builtAt} (`db/events.parquet`, `db/sites.parquet`,
`db/variables.parquet`) and `data/calculated/daily.parquet` of the same build <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. It checks
the SHA-256 and the row count of each file before it reads data (section "Reproduce"). The rules are those of Deep Dive #4
(<a href={`${base}deep-dive/disturbance-detail/`}>Disturbance detail</a>), ported to Python. All windows include both end
days. The nine rules below give each constant; open them to reproduce the numbers.

<details class="dd-table" id="method-rules">
<summary>Rules of the analysis (nine rules and the overrides)</summary>

1. **Data.** The daily GPP, TER and gap-filled NEE ({U}) and the measured fraction of NEE per local day come from
   `data/calculated/daily.parquet`. TER is the Reco column of the night-time partitioning <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />.
   The local day is the period start in UTC plus the UTC offset of the site (`utc_offset_min`, UTC+2 for all four sites). The
   events come from `db/events.parquet`.
2. **Episodes.** The events of `type` `harvest` and `cut` of each of the five Finnish sites (Anttila, Särkisuo, Haltiala,
   Qvidja, Viikki) are sorted by date. An event
   spans the days `date` to `date_end` (or `date` only). An event that starts not more
   than <Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days after the last day of the current episode joins it; the episode then
   ends on the last day of all its events. The *recorded date* is the first event date of the episode. Then the <Num k="counts.overrides" digits={0} />
   overrides of Deep Dive #4 apply (list below); they change the first or the last day of an episode. An override that
   matches no episode stops the script. The *group* of an event: a `cut` is a cleaning cut; a `harvest` whose text (the
   `crop` field, a space, the `detail` field) matches *thresh*, *grain*, *faba*, the whole word *oats*, or *barley* (any
   case) and does not match the whole word *cut*, *silage* or *grass* (any case) is a grain harvest; every other `harvest`
   is a silage harvest. The group of an episode is grain
   when one of its events is a grain harvest, else silage when one event is a harvest, else cleaning cut. The dive keeps the
   silage harvest episodes: <Num k="counts.silageEpisodes" digits={0} />.
3. **Windows and change.** From the first day (*start*) and the last day (*end*) of the episode after the overrides: before =
   start <Num k="method.beforeDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to start <Num k="method.beforeDays.1" sign digits={0} />, after = end
   <Num k="method.afterDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to end <Num k="method.afterDays.1" sign digits={0} />, late = end
   <Num k="method.lateDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to end <Num k="method.lateDays.1" sign digits={0} />. The window mean is the
   mean of the days with a value. The change Δ = mean after − mean before, for GPP, TER and NEE; it is empty when a side has
   fewer than <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with a value. The relative change of each of the three fluxes is
   100 × Δ / mean GPP before; it is empty when the mean GPP before is below <Num k="method.minGppBefore" digits={1} unit={U} />.
   Because NEE = TER − GPP, NEE % = TER % − GPP %.
4. **Exclusions**, in this order: (1) *overlap*: the span (first to last day) of another harvest or cut episode of the site,
   of any group, or the span of a grazing or tillage event, has a day in common with the days
   start <Num k="method.beforeDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to end <Num k="method.afterDays.1" sign digits={0} />
   (<Num k="counts.excluded.overlap" digits={0} /> episodes); (2) *too few days*: GPP, TER or NEE has fewer than
   <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days in the before or the after window (<Num k="counts.excluded.too-few-days" digits={0} />);
   (3) *low measured fraction*: the mean measured fraction of NEE (`frac_measured`, 0 to 1, over the days with a value) is
   below <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % in the before or the after window
   (<Num k="counts.excluded.low-measured-fraction" digits={0} />). A fertilisation in the windows only sets a flag.
   <Num k="counts.used" digits={0} /> episodes are used. Table A gives the windows, the recovery and the timing of all
   <Num k="counts.silageEpisodes" digits={0} /> episodes; the summaries and the figures use the used episodes only.
5. **Recovery.** The *next disturbance* is the smallest start of a harvest or cut episode of the site that starts after end,
   or of a grazing event that starts after end, minus end; a tillage does not count. The recovery share = 100 × mean GPP of the late window / mean GPP before; empty
   when the next disturbance starts <Num k="method.lateDays.1" digits={0} /> days or less after end, when the late window has
   fewer than <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days, or when the GPP before is below
   <Num k="method.minGppBefore" digits={1} unit={U} />. The days to recover = the first day *d* (1 to
   <Num k="method.recoveryMaxDays" digits={0} /> after end) whose centred <Num k="method.recoveryRunDays" digits={0} />-day
   running mean of GPP (<Num k="method.recoveryMinRun" digits={0} /> or more values) is
   <Num k="method.recoverySharePct" digits={0} /> % or more of the GPP before. Before each day *d* is tested, the episode is
   censored when the next disturbance comes first (*d* + 2 ≥ next, "blocked"); after a failed test it is censored when no GPP
   day remains from *d* to day <Num k="method.recoveryMaxDays" digits={0} /> + 2 ("data end"); when no day passes, it is
   censored at day <Num k="method.recoveryMaxDays" digits={0} /> ("max days").
6. **Composite curves.** Per site and over all used episodes, for the days <Num k="method.compositeFrom" sign digits={0} /> to
   <Num k="method.compositeTo" sign digits={0} /> from end (the last day of the harvest). The value of an episode is empty from
   the day end + next disturbance on. GPP, TER and NEE are a fraction of the mean GPP before of the episode (empty when it is
   below <Num k="method.minGppBefore" digits={1} unit={U} />). Per day: the mean, the sample SD and n over the episodes; the
   mean and the SD are empty when n is below <Num k="method.compositeMinN" digits={0} />. The y range of Figures 1 and 2 is one
   range over all mean ± SD of the five panels, rounded outwards to a multiple of <Num k="method.yStep" digits={2} /> and
   extended to hold 0 and 1: <Num k="composite.yRange.0" digits={2} /> to <Num k="composite.yRange.1" digits={2} />. A second
   composite over all used episodes holds the fluxes in {U} (no check of the GPP before); its y range (Figure 3) is rounded
   outwards to a multiple of <Num k="method.yStepAbs" digits={0} unit={U} /> and extended to hold 0:
   <Num k="composite.yRangeAbs.0" digits={0} /> to <Num k="composite.yRangeAbs.1" digits={0} unit={U} />.
7. **Change point.** For each candidate day *t* from start <Num k="method.searchFrom" sign digits={0} /> to end
   <Num k="method.searchTo" sign digits={0} />, drop(*t*) = mean GPP of *t* − <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> to *t* − 1
   minus mean GPP of *t* to *t* + <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> − 1, each side with
   <Num k="method.minSideGpp" digits={0} /> or more values. The detected day is the *t* with the largest drop (the first on a
   tie). The drop is *clear* when the mean of the <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> days before the detected day is
   <Num k="method.minBeforeGpp" digits={0} unit={U} /> or more and the drop is <Num k="method.minDropPct" digits={0} /> % or
   more of it. Offset = detected day − recorded date (the first event date
   before the overrides); negative = the drop came before the recorded date. An episode has no timing result when another
   harvest or cut episode, or a grazing or tillage event, overlaps start <Num k="method.spanOverlapFrom" sign digits={0} /> to
   end <Num k="method.spanOverlapTo" sign digits={0} />, or when no candidate day has enough GPP days. *Pre-drop* = 100 × (mean
   GPP of the days <Num k="method.preNear.0" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.preNear.1" sign digits={0} /> − mean of the
   days <Num k="method.preFar.0" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.preFar.1" sign digits={0} />) / mean of the days
   <Num k="method.preFar.0" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.preFar.1" sign digits={0} />, counted from the recorded date;
   the far window needs <Num k="method.preMinFar" digits={0} /> or more values and a mean of
   <Num k="method.minBeforeGpp" digits={0} unit={U} /> or more, the near window <Num k="method.preMinNear" digits={0} /> or more
   values.
8. **Statistics.** Per site and over all used episodes: n, mean, sample SD (n − 1), median (the mean of the two middle values
   for an even n) and range of ΔGPP, ΔTER and ΔNEE ({U} and %), the recovery share, the days to recover, the GPP drop offset
   (clear drops only) and the pre-drop; the counts of drops before, on and after the recorded date. The
   <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval of the mean is a percentile bootstrap
   (<Num k="method.bootstrapResamples" digits={0} /> resamples with replacement, the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles of the resampled
   means, `numpy` PCG64 with fixed seeds in the manifest) when n is 3 or more; for a smaller n the tables give the values. The
   intervals of Deep Dive #4 come from another random generator (mulberry32); all other numbers are equal.
9. **Counts and findings.** Each step that removes rows or changes values logs a count in `dataVersion.steps` of
   `results.json`. The script prints the open findings of the data report that touch this dive.

### Sites

- **Anttila: legume ley, the same ley in both data years (2022, 2023).** Sown on 3 June 2021 with red clover and timothy
  and barley as a cover crop; whole-crop harvest on 2 August 2021, before the flux data <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 2" />.
  The 2022 cuts are in Table 2 of the article <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 3, Table 2" />. The recorded date of
  the second cut of 2022 is 8 August, from the project event file `data/Agri_site_management_events 2018-2025.xlsx` (the
  event table uses the dates of this file; the article gives 8 September). The 2023 dates and the yields per cut also come
  from the project file.
- **Särkisuo: mixed grasses (timothy and meadow fescue), the same ley in both data years (2022, 2023).** Ploughed in June
  2021 and sown on 9 June 2021 with wheat, oat and grasses; whole-crop harvest in July 2021 without a recorded day, thus not
  an episode <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 2" />. The 2022 cuts are in Table 2 <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 3, Table 2" />;
  the 2023 dates and yields come from the project file.
- **Qvidja: forage grassland since 2017, the same grass from 2018 to 2024.** From 2008 to 2016 the field was in an annual
  crop rotation; in 2017 the management changed and the grass was sown in spring 2017. In June 2018 the grass was cut at
  6 cm, in the other harvests at about 15 cm; the cut of 21 August 2018 left the yield in the field, and the field was
  oversown on 3 September 2018 after a drought <Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="PDF p. 3" />. Cows grazed the
  field in August 2021 and August 2022 <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="PDF p. 2" />. The event table also has harrowing in
  April 2021, cleaning cuts in 2022 to 2024 and subsoiling in September 2023 <Cite ids={['qvidja-management']} />.
- **Viikki: a field in rotation, a different crop each year** <Cite ids={['viikki-management']} />. 2022 timothy
  <Cite ids={['viikki-management']} />, harvested in July and August <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="PDF p. 3; Table A2, PDF p. 13" />.
  2023 barley sown on 25 May with a mixed grass seed under it <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="PDF p. 3; Table A2, PDF p. 13" />;
  the management file gives the harvests on 17 and 24 August <Cite ids={['viikki-management']} /> (one episode, flag
  *establishment year*: the first year of the new ley). 2024 grass: the management file names silage with a harvest on
  31 July <Cite ids={['viikki-management']} />; the project event file `data/Agri_site_management_events 2018-2025.xlsx`
  names grass hay and adds the first cut of 14 June, where only about one hectare was harvested (flag *partial harvest*). The
  Viikki yields are year totals on the last harvest of the year.
- **Haltiala and Maktau.** The Haltiala event file has grass only as an undersown crop (2022, 2025) and as the crop of 2023,
  which was stopped by disc harrowing in August 2023; all its harvests are grain harvests <Cite ids={['haltiala-management']} />.
  Rule 2 puts grain harvests in the grain group, so Haltiala has no episode and no panel. Maktau grows maize and has no silage
  harvest, so it is not in the dive.

## Results

Each panel shows the mean of the episodes per day (lines) and ± 1 SD (bands) of GPP, TER and NEE, as a fraction of the mean
GPP of the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before the harvest. Day 0 is the last day of the harvest.
NEE = TER − GPP, thus NEE above zero is a net release. The curves are drawn only where
<Num k="method.compositeMinN" digits={0} /> or more episodes have a value. Figure 3 shows the curve of all episodes in {U}.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as UTC+2
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>; status: verified, waits for provider) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
  A day of these sites holds the true local time from <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} />:00 on that date to
  <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} />:00 on the next date, thus a daily value holds
  <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours of the next true day. A daily change point can move by not more than one
  day. A window mean of <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days changes only through its first and its last day.
- **Model values.** GPP and TER are model values of the night-time partitioning of the gap-filled NEE
  <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />, and NEE is the gap-filled NEE. Days with a low measured fraction are not removed; only a
  window with a mean measured fraction below <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % excludes an episode. This dive has
  no check on the measured NEE; Deep Dive #4 (<a href={`${base}deep-dive/disturbance-detail/`}>Disturbance detail</a>) has it.
- **Small n.** Anttila has n = <Num k="summary.perSite.anttila.n" digits={0} />, Särkisuo
  n = <Num k="summary.perSite.sarkisuo.n" digits={0} />, Qvidja n = <Num k="summary.perSite.qvidja.n" digits={0} /> and Viikki
  n = <Num k="summary.perSite.viikki.n" digits={0} /> used episodes. The bootstrap interval of a small n is not reliable; for
  n below 3 the tables give the values, not an interval.
- **Viikki episodes.** The 2023 episode is the establishment year: barley undersown with clover and grasses, cut green on two
  days (ΔGPP <Num k={`${E('viikki-2023-08-17')}.relPct.gpp`} sign digits={0} unit="%" />, ΔNEE
  <Num k={`${E('viikki-2023-08-17')}.relPct.nee`} sign digits={0} unit="%" /> of the GPP before). The first cut of 2024 harvested
  only about one hectare (ΔGPP <Num k={`${E('viikki-2024-06-14')}.relPct.gpp`} sign digits={0} unit="%" />, ΔNEE
  <Num k={`${E('viikki-2024-06-14')}.relPct.nee`} sign digits={0} unit="%" />). Both stay in the means; Table A flags them.
- **Censoring.** The composite value of an episode ends at the next harvest, cut or grazing, and the days to recover are
  censored at <Num k="method.recoveryMaxDays" digits={0} /> days (<Num k="summary.all.nCensored" digits={0} /> of
  <Num k="summary.all.n" digits={0} /> episodes). The recovery share is empty when the next disturbance comes within
  <Num k="method.lateDays.1" digits={0} /> days (n = <Num k="summary.all.recoveryShare.n" digits={0} /> of
  <Num k="summary.all.n" digits={0} />).
- **Day 0.** The composite curves count the days from the last day of the harvest. Three used episodes have more than one
  day: Qvidja 2023-06-19 and 2023-08-21 (<Num k={`${E('qvidja-2023-06-19')}.durationDays`} digits={0} /> and
  <Num k={`${E('qvidja-2023-08-21')}.durationDays`} digits={0} /> days) and Viikki 2023-08-17
  (<Num k={`${E('viikki-2023-08-17')}.durationDays`} digits={0} /> days). Their harvest days are inside the days −14 to −1 of
  the composite, and their reference stays the GPP of the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before the first day.
- **Association.** The changes are associated with the harvests. The weather, the season and the crop stage change in the
  same windows. The analysis does not show that a harvest caused a change.
- **Snapshot.** The database snapshot of this dive is not yet published as a release asset. The hashes in "Reproduce"
  identify it.
