## Short answer

The change is the mean daily NEE in the <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days after a harvest or cut minus the
mean in the <Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} /> days before. A higher NEE means less net uptake of CO₂.

For all <Num k="all.n" digits={0} /> used episodes at three sites, the mean change of NEE was
<Num k="all.nee.mean" sign unit={U} /> (<Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval
<Num k="all.nee.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="all.nee.ciHi" sign />). NEE increased in
<Num k="all.nee.nPositive" digits={0} /> of the <Num k="all.nee.n" digits={0} /> episodes. At the same time, GPP
decreased (<Num k="all.gpp.mean" sign unit={U} />, interval <Num k="all.gpp.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="all.gpp.ciHi" sign />).
The change of Reco was <Num k="all.reco.mean" sign unit={U} />, and its interval (<Num k="all.reco.ciLo" sign /> to
<Num k="all.reco.ciHi" sign />) includes zero.

- **Qvidja** (silage grassland): NEE <Num k="sites.qvidja.nee.mean" sign unit={U} /> (n = <Num k="sites.qvidja.n" digits={0} />,
  interval <Num k="sites.qvidja.nee.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="sites.qvidja.nee.ciHi" sign />). In days
  <Num k="method.lateFrom" digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.lateTo" digits={0} /> after the event, the change was
  <Num k="sites.qvidja.late.nee.mean" sign unit={U} /> (n = <Num k="sites.qvidja.late.nee.n" digits={0} />, interval
  <Num k="sites.qvidja.late.nee.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="sites.qvidja.late.nee.ciHi" sign />).
- **Viikki** (grass and crop rotation): NEE <Num k="sites.viikki.nee.mean" sign unit={U} /> (n = <Num k="sites.viikki.n" digits={0} />,
  interval <Num k="sites.viikki.nee.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="sites.viikki.nee.ciHi" sign />). The mean is positive.
  <Num k="sites.viikki.n" digits={0} /> episodes are too few for a conclusion.
- **Haltiala** (cereal field, grain harvests at the end of the season): NEE <Num k="sites.haltiala.nee.mean" sign unit={U} />
  (n = <Num k="sites.haltiala.n" digits={0} />, interval <Num k="sites.haltiala.nee.ciLo" sign /> to
  <Num k="sites.haltiala.nee.ciHi" sign />). The interval includes zero.

The data show no significant relation between the yield of a harvest and the change of NEE.

## Data and method

**Events.** We used the harvest and cut events of the management files
<Cite ids={['qvidja-management', 'viikki-management', 'haltiala-management']} />. The build stores them in
`db/events.parquet`. There are <Num k="counts.harvests" digits={0} /> harvests and <Num k="counts.cuts" digits={0} /> cuts.
A harvest removes the biomass. At Qvidja, a cut is a cleaning cut, and the grass stays on the field
<Cite ids={['qvidja-management']} />.

**Episodes.** We sorted the events of each site by date. An event joins the current episode when it starts not more
than <Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days after the end of that episode. The end of an episode is the last
day of its events. Where the file gives an end date (`date_end`), we used it. For example, the Haltiala harvest of
2022-08-15 ends on 2022-08-24. This gives <Num k="counts.episodes" digits={0} /> episodes from
<Num k="counts.events" digits={0} /> events. The yield of an episode is the sum of the yields of its events. In this
data, not more than one event per episode has a yield.

**Fluxes.** We used the daily sums of gap-filled NEE, GPP and Reco in g C m⁻² d⁻¹ from
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. A day is the local calendar day (`date_local`).
NEE > 0 is a net release of CO₂ to the air. GPP ≥ 0 is the uptake by photosynthesis. The data providers made the
gap-filled NEE. This project made GPP and Reco with the night-time partitioning. In this method, Reco is a temperature
model fitted to night NEE. GPP is Reco − NEE.

**Windows.** For each episode we calculated the mean daily flux in these windows:

- *before*: the <Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} /> days before the first day of the episode,
- *after*: the <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days after the last day of the episode (main result),
- *early*: days one to <Num k="method.earlyDays" digits={0} /> after the last day,
- *late*: days <Num k="method.lateFrom" digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.lateTo" digits={0} /> after the last day.

The days of the episode are not in a window. The change of an episode is the mean of a window after the episode
minus the mean of the *before* window.

**Rules.** An episode is used when it passes all three rules:

- **Rule 1.** No other harvest or cut episode of the site starts in the *before* window or in the *after* window.
- **Rule 2.** Each of the two windows has at least <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with all three fluxes.
- **Rule 3.** In each of the two windows, the mean measured fraction of NEE is at least
  <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> %. The measured fraction is the mean of `frac_measured` of the days with
  all three fluxes.

The late window has the same day rule as rule 2. It also must not contain the start of another episode. Else the
late value of the episode is empty.

We added rule 3 for this reason: the gap-filling models use data from the days before and after the event. When most
half hours are gap-filled, the filled values can hide the change. <Num k="counts.excluded" digits={0} /> episodes
did not pass rule 3. All episodes passed rules 1 and 2. <Num k="counts.used" digits={0} /> episodes are used.
These episodes are not used:

<ul>{excluded.map((t) => <li>{t}</li>)}</ul>

**Statistics.** We calculated these values per site and for all sites:

- the mean change and the standard deviation (SD),
- the number of episodes (n),
- a <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % percentile bootstrap interval of the mean
  (<Num k="method.bootstrapResamples" digits={0} /> resamples, fixed seed).

For the sites with few episodes (Viikki, Haltiala), a bootstrap interval is not reliable. Use the single values in the table.

**Check without gap filling.** We also used the *measured* half-hourly NEE in µmol m⁻² s⁻¹. A day is the local date
of `tl` in `data/calculated/fluxes_30min.parquet`. We used the night flag of the partitioning (low radiation) to make
two groups: day and night. Half hours without a night flag (<Num k="counts.nightNullPct" digits={1} unit="%" /> of the measured half hours) are not in
the day group or in the night group. For each window we calculated the mean measured NEE of each group. A window needs at least
<Num k="method.minMeasuredDay" digits={0} /> measured day-time half hours or <Num k="method.minMeasuredNight" digits={0} />
measured night-time half hours. Day and night have a different number of hours. This check gives the direction of
the change, not a daily sum.

**Yield.** We used the dry-matter yield of the episode in t DW ha⁻¹. Qvidja and Haltiala give a yield per harvest.
Viikki gives one yield per year on the last harvest of the year. We used the Viikki value only when the year has one
episode. We calculated the Pearson correlation r, its two-sided p-value (t-test, n − 2 degrees of freedom) and the
least-squares slope. The slope has the same p-value as r.

**Comparison with the articles.** Heimsch et al. compared five-day means of NEE before and after each harvest at
Qvidja. The harvest of June 2018 changed the mean CO₂ flux from a net sink to a small source
<Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="p. 3473" />. This harvest used a cutting height of 6 cm. The other harvests of
2018 and 2019 used 15 cm <Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="PDF p. 3" />. After the harvests with 15 cm, the mean sink
rate decreased to near zero <Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="p. 3473" />.

Shurpali et al. measured three grassland fields in Finland. After each harvest, the fields changed to a short net
release of CO₂ <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 4" />. The fields were sources until the regrowth and the
increasing GPP were larger than the respiration <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 11" />. Our result agrees with these two articles.

At Qvidja, Heimsch et al. report that the highest yield (June 2021) did not give a high annual GPP. In their data,
the leaf area did not recover after that harvest <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="PDF p. 6" />. Vira et al. compared NPP from the yields with NPP from GPP. For
the grasslands, the two estimates were not significantly correlated <Cite ids={['vira2025']} note="PDF p. 14, PDF p. 16" />.

The carbon in the harvest is a separate export term of the net ecosystem carbon balance (NECB). Hu et al. use this
term in a model framework (DailyDayCent) <Cite ids={['hu2024']} note="p. 8" />. A model study of Qvidja found that the
management events tended to change the NECB more through the import and export of carbon than through a change of NEE
<Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="PDF p. 9" />. This dive does not include the export term. It shows only the change
of the CO₂ exchange.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Small n.** Viikki and Haltiala have only <Num k="sites.viikki.n" digits={0} /> and
  <Num k="sites.haltiala.n" digits={0} /> used episodes. Their bootstrap intervals are not reliable. Use the single
  values in the table. At Qvidja the SD of the change of NEE is <Num k="sites.qvidja.nee.sd" unit={U} />
  (n = <Num k="sites.qvidja.n" digits={0} />).
- **Season.** The windows also contain the normal change of the season. In August and September, GPP also decreases
  without a harvest. We have no control field without a harvest. The change is *associated with* the event. This analysis
  does not show that the event causes all of the change. The Haltiala harvests were at the end of the season.
- **Other management.** <Num k="counts.withOtherEvents" digits={0} /> used episodes have other events in the windows.
  In <Num k="counts.withFertilisation" digits={0} /> of them, a fertilisation follows the harvest (see the table). We
  did not remove these episodes.
- **Gap filling.** In the used windows, the mean measured fraction of NEE is <Num k="counts.fracMeasuredMeanPct" digits={0} /> %
  (lowest <Num k="counts.fracMeasuredMinPct" digits={0} /> %). The other half hours are gap-filled. The gap-filling
  models do not use the harvest date. They can make the change smaller. GPP and Reco are model values. The
  reference respiration Rref of the Reco model comes from windows of <Num k="method.rrefWindowDays" digits={0} /> days,
  every <Num k="method.rrefStepDays" digits={0} /> days <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />.
- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as local time (UTC+2). The
  Viikki values are 2 hours early (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>finding VII-01</a>, waits for
  provider). We tested this. We made the Viikki daily sums again from the 30-min data with the local time moved by
  <Num k="utcShift.hours" digits={0} /> hours. The largest difference in a change of NEE, GPP or Reco of an episode was
  <Num k="utcShift.maxAbsChange" unit={U} />. With the moved time, the mean change of NEE at Viikki is
  <Num k="utcShift.nee.mean" sign unit={U} /> (without: <Num k="sites.viikki.nee.mean" sign />).
- **Viikki gap filling.** The gap-filled NEE of Viikki in 2024 differs from the measured NEE in some periods
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-05`}>finding VII-05</a>) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
- **Qvidja annual NEE.** The annual NEE of Qvidja in our data is more negative than in Heimsch et al. 2024
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-11`}>finding QVI-11</a>). We did not test the effect of this difference
  on the change around an event.
- **Event dates.** We used the dates of the management files. Heimsch et al. 2021 give some dates that are one or two
  days different: 23 September 2018 (file: 2018-09-25), 11 June 2019 (file: 2019-06-10) and 20 August 2019 (file:
  2019-08-21) <Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="PDF p. 3" />. A harvest can take more than one day. We used the first
  and the last day where the file gives them.

## Reproduce

The numbers on this page come from `site/src/deep-dives/harvest-carbon.json`. The script
[`scripts/deep-dives/harvest-carbon.ts`](https://github.com/RJuho/inar-agri/blob/main/scripts/deep-dives/harvest-carbon.ts)
writes this file. It reads only `db/events.parquet`, `db/sites.parquet`, `data/calculated/daily.parquet` and
`data/calculated/fluxes_30min.parquet`.

```sh
npm run build:db && npm run calc      # make the inputs
npm run deep-dives -- harvest-carbon  # write the JSON again
npm run deep-dives -- --check         # compare a fresh run with the committed JSON
```
