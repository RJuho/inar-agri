## Short answer

We estimated two losses of net CO₂ uptake. The *harvest deficit* H uses a model of the canopy before a cut. The model
gives the uptake that this canopy would have had in the weather after the cut. H is that uptake minus the observed
uptake. The *drought anomaly* D compares a dry month with the same calendar month of years that were not dry. First we
remove H from the NEE of the month. D is that NEE minus the mean NEE of the non-dry years with the same crop class. An NEE below zero is a net uptake. A positive H or D is uptake that was lost. The NEE without harvest
and drought is NEE − H − D.

- **Grass cuts.** In the <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days after a cut, the mean harvest deficit was
  <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.d28.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} /> at Qvidja (95 % CI
  <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.d28.ciLo" digits={1} /> to <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.d28.ciHi" digits={1} />,
  SD <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.d28.sd" digits={1} />, n = <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.d28.n" digits={0} /> episodes).
  The grass cuts at Viikki gave <Num k="harvest.bySiteCrop.viikki.grass.d28.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} />
  (95 % CI <Num k="harvest.bySiteCrop.viikki.grass.d28.ciLo" digits={1} /> to
  <Num k="harvest.bySiteCrop.viikki.grass.d28.ciHi" digits={1} />, SD
  <Num k="harvest.bySiteCrop.viikki.grass.d28.sd" digits={1} />, n = <Num k="harvest.bySiteCrop.viikki.grass.d28.n" digits={0} />
  cuts). With so few cuts, the interval is only the range of the values. The barley harvest of Viikki gave
  <Num k="episodes.viikki-2023-08-17.d28" digits={1} sign unit={U} /> and the faba bean harvest
  <Num k="episodes.viikki-2025-09-10.d28" digits={1} sign unit={U} />.
- **Cereal harvests** gave a deficit near zero (mean <Num k="harvest.byCrop.cereal.d28.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} />,
  SD <Num k="harvest.byCrop.cereal.d28.sd" digits={1} />, n = <Num k="harvest.byCrop.cereal.d28.n" digits={0} />). The Haltiala
  cereals were harvested at the end of the growing season <Cite ids={['ke2025']} note="p. 5" />. The light-saturated GPP of
  the model before these harvests was low (see the Haltiala section), thus the model expected little uptake.
- **Dry months** (SPI-1 ≤ <Num k="method.dryThreshold" digits={0} />) gave no consistent loss. At Qvidja the mean anomaly
  of the dry months was <Num k="dry.summary.qvidja.anomaly.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} />
  (n = <Num k="dry.summary.qvidja.anomaly.n" digits={0} />, SD <Num k="dry.summary.qvidja.anomaly.sd" digits={1} />). These
  months had a little more uptake. At Haltiala it was <Num k="dry.summary.haltiala.anomaly.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} />
  (n = <Num k="dry.summary.haltiala.anomaly.n" digits={0} />, SD <Num k="dry.summary.haltiala.anomaly.sd" digits={1} />). At
  Viikki it was <Num k="dry.summary.viikki.anomaly.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} />
  (n = <Num k="dry.summary.viikki.anomaly.n" digits={0} />, SD <Num k="dry.summary.viikki.anomaly.sd" digits={1} />). The
  spread is large at all three sites. The regressions of the monthly NEE anomaly on SPI-1 had no clear slope at the three sites.
- **Seasons.** Over the <Num k="allSites.totals.all.nSeasons" digits={0} /> complete seasons of the three sites, H added up to
  <Num k="allSites.totals.all.harvestDeficit" digits={0} sign unit={U} /> and D to
  <Num k="allSites.totals.all.droughtAnomaly" digits={0} sign unit={U} />. The harvest part was
  <Num k="allSites.totals.all.shareHarvestAbs" digits={0} percent /> of the size of both parts. Most of H was in summer
  (mean <Num k="allSites.bySeason.JJA.harvestDeficit.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} /> per site-summer, 95 % CI
  <Num k="allSites.bySeason.JJA.harvestDeficit.ciLo" digits={1} /> to <Num k="allSites.bySeason.JJA.harvestDeficit.ciHi" digits={1} />,
  SD <Num k="allSites.bySeason.JJA.harvestDeficit.sd" digits={1} />, n = <Num k="allSites.bySeason.JJA.n" digits={0} />).

This dive is different from the other dives. [Deep Dive #0](../harvest-carbon/) gives the change of the daily fluxes
in the two weeks after a harvest. [Deep Dive #1](../disturbance-size/) compares the daily NEE after a cut with the mean
of the days before it. [Deep Dive #2](../natural-events/) compares dry-spell days with other days of the same months.
This dive uses a model of the canopy before the cut as the reference. The light and the temperature after the cut drive
this model. We add the losses per month and per season. We also use a drought index of the weather stations.

## Data and method

**Sites and data.** Haltiala (cereals, one failed grass year and one sunflower year), Qvidja (grass for silage) and
Viikki (grass, a barley and clover mixture, and faba bean). We used the 30-minute gap-filled NEE (`nee_f`), the GPP and
Reco of the night-time partitioning, the air temperature of the partitioning and the E0 of each row from
`data/calculated/fluxes_30min.parquet`, and the daily NEE sums (`nee_f_gc`) of `data/calculated/daily.parquet`
<Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. In these files NEE = Reco − GPP in each row. The light driver is the photosynthetic
photon flux density at Haltiala and Viikki, and the incoming shortwave radiation at Qvidja (Qvidja has no PPFD
sensor). Days are local days of the site.

**Harvest and cut episodes.** We used the harvest and cut events of the management files
<Cite ids={['haltiala-management', 'qvidja-management', 'viikki-management']} />. Events that are not more than
<Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days apart make one episode. This gives
<Num k="counts.episodes" digits={0} /> episodes: <Num k="sites.haltiala.episodes" digits={0} /> at Haltiala,
<Num k="sites.qvidja.episodes" digits={0} /> at Qvidja and <Num k="sites.viikki.episodes" digits={0} /> at Viikki. Haltiala
has no harvest in 2023 (failed grass) and 2024 (sunflower). The Qvidja file has no harvest in 2025, only grazing.
Ke et al. describe Qvidja as cut in June and August and Haltiala as harvested one time at the end of the season
<Cite ids={['ke2025']} note="p. 5" />.

**Harvest deficit.** For each episode we fitted a model on the <Num k="method.preDays" digits={0} /> days before the
first cut day (PRE):

- GPP is a rectangular hyperbola of light. We fitted it on the mean GPP of light bins. A bin is
  <Num k="method.lightBin.haltiala" digits={0} /> µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ PPFD or <Num k="method.lightBin.qvidja" digits={0} /> W m⁻²
  shortwave. We used only light above <Num k="method.lightMin" digits={0} />. The fit needs
  <Num k="method.minBins" digits={0} /> or more bins.
- Reco is the Lloyd–Taylor function with the E0 of the partitioning. We fitted only the reference respiration.
- The fit needs <Num k="method.minPreDays" digits={0} /> or more PRE days with data.

Heimsch et al. used the same two functions for Qvidja <Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="p. 5" />. The model gives the
NEE that the canopy before the cut would have had in the light and the temperature of each half hour after the cut. For each
day we took the mean of (observed NEE − model NEE) over the half hours of the day. The daily deficit is this mean times
<Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours, in g C m⁻² d⁻¹. A day needs
<Num k="method.minHalfHours" digits={0} /> or more half hours. The window after the cut starts on the day after the
last cut day. It is <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days long (H28). It is shorter when the next episode starts
earlier. The window needs <Num k="method.minValidShare" digits={0} percent /> valid days. A missing day gets the mean of
the valid days. NEE = Reco − GPP, thus the deficit splits exactly into two parts. The GPP part is model GPP − observed
GPP. The Reco part is observed Reco − model Reco. We also found a *recovery day* for each
episode. It is the first day from day <Num k="method.recovery.fromDay" digits={0} /> on that meets this condition: the mean
deficit of <Num k="method.recovery.span" digits={0} /> days is at or below
<Num k="method.recovery.share" digits={0} percent /> of the mean deficit of the first
<Num k="method.recovery.fromDay" digits={0} /> days.

**Drought index.** The project team computed the Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI) of the FMI stations Vantaa, Kaarina Yltöinen and Kuopio Maaninka from the
monthly precipitation <Cite ids={['spi-data']} />. The project team used the `spi()` function of the SPEI R package
with the reference period 1981 to 2010 and the defaults of the function (gamma distribution, unbiased
probability-weighted moments). Vantaa (Helsinki-Vantaa airport) is about
<Num k="method.stationKm.haltiala" digits={0} unit="km" /> from Haltiala and <Num k="method.stationKm.viikki" digits={0} unit="km" />
from Viikki. Kaarina Yltöinen is about <Num k="method.stationKm.qvidja" digits={0} unit="km" /> from Qvidja. A month is
*dry* when SPI-1 ≤ <Num k="method.dryThreshold" digits={0} />. A season is dry when one or more of its months is dry.

**Drought anomaly.** For each month from March to November with <Num k="method.minMonthDays" digits={0} /> or more days of
daily NEE, we removed the harvest deficit of the days in that month: NEE_adj = NEE − H. We remove the harvest
first. The harvest model uses the observed weather of the month, so H is the harvest effect in this weather. The anomaly
of NEE_adj is then the drought effect without the harvest. The sum is exact: NEE without both = NEE − H − D. The anomaly D of a dry month
is NEE_adj minus the mean NEE_adj of the same calendar month in the non-dry years of the site with the same crop class.
When fewer than <Num k="method.minRefYears" digits={0} /> such years exist, we used all non-dry years; with fewer than
<Num k="method.minRefYears" digits={0} /> of them, the month has no anomaly. Qvidja 2025 (harvest not known) is not used
as a reference. A month with missing days is scaled to the full month.

**Regression.** For each site we regressed the monthly anomaly (NEE_adj minus the mean of the same calendar month of
the site) on SPI-1, with a bootstrap 95 % confidence interval of the slope
(<Num k="method.bootstrapResamplesSlope" digits={0} /> resamples). At Qvidja we also regressed the seasonal sum of NEE_adj
on the mean SPI-1 of the season.

**Seasons.** Spring (MAM), summer (JJA) and autumn (SON) per site and year. A season is complete when its three months
are complete. The totals and the all-sites summaries use the complete seasons with a known harvest record. Means have a
bootstrap 95 % CI (<Num k="method.bootstrapResamplesMean" digits={0} /> resamples).

## Limits and uncertainty

- **The harvest deficit can be too large.** The model keeps the canopy of the PRE days for the full window. A grass canopy
  that was not cut would also age, and its uptake would change. At Qvidja the leaf area of 2021 did not recover after the
  June harvest <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="p. 6" />. The window after the cut is limited to
  <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days. A loss after that is not in H, and a deficit inside the cut days of a
  multi-day episode is not in H.
- **Model fit.** The light response and the reference respiration come from <Num k="method.preDays" digits={0} /> days only.
  A low reference respiration before the cut gives a large Reco part after it, for example June 2018 and June 2023 at
  Qvidja (see the episode table). The GPP and Reco after the cut come from the night-time partitioning
  <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />, not from measurements. We did not use the GPP and Reco of the data provider
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-07`}>finding QVI-07</a>).
- **Gap-filled NEE.** Only <Num k="sites.qvidja.measuredShareAfter.mean" digits={0} percent /> (mean) of the half hours after
  the cuts at Qvidja and <Num k="sites.viikki.measuredShareAfter.mean" digits={0} percent /> at Viikki were measured. With the
  measured half hours only, the mean deficit was <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.measuredD28.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} />
  at Qvidja (95 % CI <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.measuredD28.ciLo" digits={1} /> to
  <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.measuredD28.ciHi" digits={1} />, n = <Num k="harvest.bySite.qvidja.measuredD28.n" digits={0} />)
  and <Num k="harvest.bySite.viikki.measuredD28.mean" digits={1} sign unit={U} /> at Viikki
  (n = <Num k="harvest.bySite.viikki.measuredD28.n" digits={0} />). Single episodes can differ much (episode table). The
  annual NEE of Qvidja is more negative than in the article of Heimsch et al.
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-11`}>finding QVI-11</a>).
- **Small references.** June at Kaarina was dry in most years, thus the Qvidja June reference has
  n = <Num k="dry.months.qvidja.2022-06.refN" digits={0} />. At Haltiala and Viikki most references have only
  <Num k="method.minRefYears" digits={0} /> years (see the table of the dry site-months). One year more or less can change D much. The regression uses all
  months and is the stronger test.
- **Crop and phenology.** At Haltiala and Viikki the crop changes from year to year. A dry month of a cereal year is
  compared with other cereal years only when there are enough of them, else with all crops. Thus D also contains the
  differences between the crops and their stages (for example Haltiala June, Viikki April 2023).
- **Drought index.** SPI uses only precipitation. SPEI also uses the evaporation. Heimsch et al. used SPEI, and
  their classes are different from ours <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="p. 5" />. The stations are about <Num k="method.stationKm.haltiala" digits={0} /> to
  <Num k="method.stationKm.qvidja" digits={0} unit="km" /> from the sites, and summer rain is local. The project team used the
  `spi()` function of the SPEI R package with the reference period 1981 to 2010 and the defaults of the function (gamma
  distribution, unbiased probability-weighted moments) <Cite ids={['spi-data']} />. The PNG files in `data/SPI/` cut the bars at plus and minus three; the CSV file has the full values. We did not
  use soil water, because the soil water of Haltiala 2021 is not comparable with the later years
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-HAL-03`}>finding HAL-03</a>). The Qvidja rain gauge has a gap from December 2021 to
  May 2022 (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-08`}>finding QVI-08</a>), thus we used the station. The humidity of
  Qvidja before October 2021 has a dry bias (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-04`}>finding QVI-04</a>); VPD is not
  an input of our model, but it can affect the gap filling.
- **Unknown harvests.** The Qvidja management file has no harvest in 2025. We kept 2025 out of the references and the
  totals. Haltiala 2023 was a failed grass crop without a harvest.
- **Time stamps.** The raw stamps of Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as local time, thus the values are two
  hours early (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>finding VII-01</a>). For monthly and seasonal sums this is
  not important. For the deficit, the time error can move some half hours
  after a cut to the wrong day.

## Reproduce

The numbers on this page come from `site/src/deep-dives/nee-without-harvest-drought.json`. The script
[`scripts/deep-dives/nee-without-harvest-drought.ts`](https://github.com/RJuho/inar-agri/blob/main/scripts/deep-dives/nee-without-harvest-drought.ts)
writes this file. It reads only `db/sites.parquet`, `db/events.parquet`, `data/calculated/daily.parquet`,
`data/calculated/fluxes_30min.parquet` and `data/SPI/SPI_all_stations.csv`. The pure helpers are in
`scripts/lib/deep-dive/counterfactual.ts` and `scripts/lib/deep-dive/spi.ts` (unit tests `counterfactual.test.ts` and
`spi.test.ts`).

```sh
npm run build:db && npm run calc                     # make the inputs
npm run deep-dives -- nee-without-harvest-drought    # write the JSON again
npm run deep-dives -- --check                        # compare a fresh run with the committed JSON
```
