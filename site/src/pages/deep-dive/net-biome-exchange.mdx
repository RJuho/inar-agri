## Short answer

Net biome exchange (NBE) is NEE plus the carbon that management takes out of the field (C_out), minus the carbon that it
brings in (C_in). At the six sites, <Num k={`${FS}.nSourceNee`} digits={0} /> of the <Num k={`${FS}.nbe.n`} digits={0} />
site-years in Figure 1 had NEE above zero, and <Num k={`${FS}.nSourceNbe`} digits={0} /> had NBE above zero. The carbon
of harvest and grazing changed <Num k={`${FS}.nSinkNeeSourceNbe`} digits={0} /> net CO₂ sinks into carbon losses. For
<Num k={`${FS}.nSinkToSourceRangeInclZero`} digits={0} /> of them, <E site="qvidja" />Qvidja 2025, NBE is less than 1 g C m⁻² yr⁻¹
(<V k="qvidja-2025" f="nbe" n={1} sign />), and its range includes zero. Organic inputs changed
<Num k={`${FS}.nSourceNeeSinkNbe`} digits={0} /> net CO₂ sources into carbon gains; for
<Num k={`${FS}.nSourceToSinkRangeInclZero`} digits={0} /> of them, <E site="viikki" />Viikki 2024, the range includes zero.
At Qvidja in 2023, a compost input made NBE strongly negative. At the <E site="hyytiala" />Hyytiälä forest, the wood of
the 2020 thinning made NBE <Num k="summary.hyytiala.thinning.nbe" digits={0} sign unit={UA} />. NBE includes no CH₄, and some terms are assumptions.

## Data and method

**Formula and sign.** NBE = NEE + C_out − C_in, in {UA}. A positive NBE is a carbon loss from the field (the ecosystem),
the same sign as NEE. NBE contains CO₂-C only; CH₄ is not in it. One site-year is one site and one local calendar year.

**Data.** The script `analysis/net-biome-exchange/net-biome-exchange.py` reads the database snapshot built on
{d.dataVersion.snapshot.builtAt} and two calculated tables of the same build <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. The
annual table gives the gap-filled annual NEE and the annual GPP and TER (= Reco) of the night-time partitioning. A
site-year without an annual NEE sum has no NBE. The management table gives the carbon of each management event in g C m⁻²,
from the management files <Cite ids={['haltiala-management', 'qvidja-management', 'viikki-management']} /> and the
articles. An event belongs to the calendar year of its date. A harvest row is C_out; this includes the grass that grazing
animals eat. A fertilisation row with carbon is C_in; this includes the excreta of the grazing animals. A row with a
carbon fraction from the site is site data. Each other row gets an assumption (next paragraph). The script stops when
such a row has no assumption.

**Assumptions.** Each assumption has a central value and a value for the lowest and the highest NBE. The values come from
articles with a page (table "Values from the literature"), or from the management files (e, h, i). A value in kg ha⁻¹ divided
by 10 is in g m⁻²; a value in Mg ha⁻¹ times 100 is in g m⁻².

- a. Viikki crop carbon ({assumptionSiteYears(d, 'a-viikki-crop-c')}): dry mass times <L k="cropCGao" n={2} />, the value
  that Gao et al. use for this field <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="p. 7" />. Low NBE: <L k="cropCHeimsch" n={2} />
  <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="p. 4" />. High NBE: <L k="cropCShurpaliMax" n={2} />, the highest grass carbon
  content of Shurpali et al. <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 8, Table 4" />.
- b. Manure carbon fraction of dry mass (DM): the organic fraction (<L k="gaoOrganicFraction" n={3} />) times the carbon in
  the organic matter (<L k="gaoCOfOrganic" n={3} />) of the dry manure of the Viikki field, that is
  <Num k="derived.manureCFraction" digits={4} /> g C per g DM <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="p. 7" />.
- c. Viikki 2023 dry manure: <L k="gaoManureDm" /> kg DM/ha times the fraction above <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="p. 7" />.
  This gives the <L k="gaoCManure2023" /> kg C/ha of Gao et al. <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="p. 6, Table 2" />. Low NBE
  (more input): <L k="vettVolumeSf" /> m³/ha of solid fraction with <L k="vettDensitySf" /> kg m⁻³ and <L k="vettDmSf" n={3} />
  DM <Cite ids={['vettikkat2026']} note="p. 2, Table 1; p. 3, Table 2" />. High NBE: the central value.
- d. Viikki 2024 manure: dry manure <Num k="derived.viikkiN2024.dry manure" digits={1} /> kg N/ha (9 October) and liquid
  manure <Num k="derived.viikkiN2024.liquid manure" digits={1} /> kg N/ha. The management file gives kg N/ha only. Its 2023 value, <Num k="derived.viikkiN2023" digits={1} /> kg N/ha,
  agrees with the ammoniacal N (TAN) of Vettikkat et al., <L k="vettTanApplied" n={1} /> kg/ha <Cite ids={['vettikkat2026']} note="p. 2, Table 1" />.
  The script therefore reads the N of the file as TAN. Dry manure: central and high NBE with the DM per kg N of 2023,
  <L k="gaoManureDm" /> kg DM / <Num k="derived.viikkiN2023" digits={1} /> kg N (Gao et al. and the management file); low NBE with the DM per kg TAN of the solid fraction. Liquid manure: central with the DM per kg TAN of the
  liquid fraction; low NBE with the raw manure per kg TAN; high NBE with the liquid fraction per kg total N
  <Cite ids={['vettikkat2026']} note="p. 3, Table 2" />. Carbon = DM times the manure fraction.
- e. Haltiala 2023 manure, <Num k="derived.haltialaVolume" digits={0} /> m³/ha of an unknown type. DM per m³: central the raw manure of Vettikkat et al., low NBE
  the solid fraction, high NBE the liquid fraction <Cite ids={['vettikkat2026']} note="p. 3, Table 2" />. Low NBE also
  uses <Num k="derived.haltialaVolumeMax" digits={0} /> m³/ha: the event note says the volume "could be 45-60 m3/ha"
  <Cite ids={['haltiala-management']} />. The applications on the two sectors (June and August) count as one
  application on the field, as in the management table.
- f. Qvidja 2020 own NPK mix (DM known, carbon not known). Heimsch et al. give an organic input of
  <L k="heimschInput2020" /> ± <Num k="literatureByKey.heimschInput2020.uncertainty" digits={0} /> {UA} for 4 May 2020 to
  3 May 2021 <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="p. 6, Table 1" />. Their input includes the liquid fertilisers
  <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="p. 4" />. The NPK mix is this total minus the compost row of the same window
  (<Num k="derived.qvidjaKnownInput2020" digits={0} /> {UA}). Low NBE (more input): total plus the uncertainty, minus the
  compost row. High NBE: total minus the uncertainty, minus the compost row, but not below zero.
- g. Qvidja 2021 broiler litter: DM times the manure fraction (a value for dry cattle manure; broiler litter can differ).
  Low NBE: DM times <L k="gaoCOfOrganic" n={3} /> (all DM organic). High NBE: no carbon, because the input of Heimsch et al.
  for 4 May 2021 to 3 May 2022 is only <L k="heimschInput2021" /> {UA} <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="p. 6, Table 1" />.
- h. Qvidja 2023 compost fibre: the carbon fraction of the management file (<Num k="derived.compostFibreCFraction" digits={2} /> of DM). The range is the carbon fraction
  of the other Qvidja composts with site data, <Num k="derived.compostCFractions.0" digits={4} /> to
  <Num k="derived.compostCFractions.1" digits={4} /> (the carbon in g C m⁻² times 10, divided by the dry mass in kg ha⁻¹, of the management table; the script uses the unrounded fractions).
- i. Qvidja 2025 grazing (no carbon values): animal-days = animals times days, from the event text. Each quantified
  Qvidja grazing row gives a removal rate and an excreta rate per animal-day (carbon divided by the animal-days of the
  row). Central: the mean rate of the horse rows (2021) for the horses, and the mean rate of the cattle rows (2022 and
  2024) for the cattle. Low NBE: the removal and excreta rates of the row with the smallest net rate (removal minus
  excreta), on all animal-days of the year. High NBE: the rates of the row with the largest net rate, on all
  animal-days. The rows are per field area, as in the file.

**Classes and range.** Class A: a full year, and all terms are site data. Class B: one or more assumed terms, or a partial
year. A partial year has no range. Low NBE uses the low-NBE value of each assumed term, and high NBE the high-NBE value. All assumptions go to their
extremes together, so the range is wide; it is not a confidence interval. The range does not include the uncertainty of NEE.

**Maktau.** The management table has no Maktau row. Räsänen et al. cite a maize yield of <L k="maktauYield2021" n={2} /> t/ha
for the dry year 2021 and a complete crop failure in 2022 <Cite ids={['rasanen2026']} note="p. 11" />. They give no
residue fate and no yield of the other years. Maktau therefore has no NBE.

**Hyytiälä forest.** The script reads the SMEAR II Hyytiälä years 2019 to 2024 as in Deep Dive #9: gap-filled NEE and GPP
of table HYY_EDDY233, TER = NEE + GPP, local time UTC+2, and the measured CO₂ flux at 27 m (F_c_270)
<Cite ids={['smear-hyytiala', 'kulmala2019']} />. An annual sum is empty when one half hour has no value. The measured
fraction is the share of the half hours with an F_c_270 value; the longest gap is the longest run of half hours without
one (a missing value in the file). The full-year and reliable rules are the same as for the fields. The stand
was thinned in February and March 2020; about <L k="thinEarlyAreaHa" /> ha was thinned in December 2019
and January 2020, and the script puts all of it in 2020 <Cite ids={['aalto-thinning2023']} note="p. 2" />. C_out of 2020
is the commercial removal (logs and pulpwood), mean of the plots in the 200 m radius of the tower:
<L k="thinRemoval200m" n={1} /> Mg C ha⁻¹, that is <Num k="summary.hyytiala.thinning.cOut" digits={0} /> {UA} <Cite ids={['aalto-thinning2023']} note="p. 7, Table 3" />. About
<L k="thinShare200mPct" /> % of the forest in this radius was thinned <Cite ids={['aalto-thinning2023']} note="p. 1" />.
Low NBE uses the mean of all 43 plots (<L k="thinRemovalAllPlots" n={1} />), high NBE the 26 thinned plots
(<L k="thinRemovalThinnedPlots" n={1} />). The report converts dry mass to carbon with <L k="thinCarbonFraction" n={1} />
<Cite ids={['aalto-thinning2023']} note="p. 6" />. The cutting residue (<L k="thinResidue200m" n={1} /> Mg C ha⁻¹
<Cite ids={['aalto-thinning2023']} note="p. 7, Table 3" />) was left at the site <Cite ids={['aalto-thinning2023']} note="p. 3" />
and is not an output. The understorey clearing of
spring 2019 left all trees on the ground <Cite ids={['aalto-thinning2023']} note="p. 2" />. The script uses no other
output or input for 2019 to 2024, so NBE = NEE in these years. De Quesada et al. give a similar removal,
<Num k="literatureByKey.dequesadaRemoval.value" digits={1} /> ± <Num k="literatureByKey.dequesadaRemoval.uncertainty" digits={1} /> kg C m⁻²,
and their NEP does not include it <Cite ids={['dequesada2025']} note="p. 3; p. 7" />.

**Figure.** The x axis is the annual GPP. The y axis is the annual carbon out, TER + C_out − C_in. Thus y − x = NBE, and the
dotted 1:1 line is NBE = 0. A site-year is in the figure when it is a full year, it is reliable (measured fraction
<Num k="method.minFrac" digits={1} /> or more, longest gap <Num k="method.maxGapDays" digits={0} /> days or less), it has
annual GPP and TER sums (both not empty), and it has an NBE. This is the rule of Figure 4 of Deep Dive #9. The summary values use the
site-years of the six sites in the figure, without Hyytiälä: n, mean, sample SD, minimum and maximum. There is no test;
n is small and the site-years of one site are not independent.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Assumed terms.** Viikki has no class-A year: the crop carbon fraction is not measured there. The manure terms of
  Viikki and Haltiala come from DM and N values of one manure analysis on the Viikki farm, not of the applied lots. The
  ranges show the effect of these choices only roughly.
- **Partial years.** Haltiala 2021 starts on 7 May and Qvidja 2018 on 9 May. Their NEE, C_out, C_in and NBE are sums over
  the measured part of the year, not annual values. They are not in the figure.
- **NEE uncertainty.** The range does not include the uncertainty of the annual NEE. Many half hours are gap-filled,
  and the annual sums depend on the gap-filling model <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />.
- **Fate of the carbon.** NBE counts the carbon of an input when it comes in and the carbon of a harvest when it goes out.
  It does not say how much of an input stays in the soil, or when the harvested carbon goes back to the atmosphere.
- **Yield data.** Haltiala 2025 yield may be a fresh weight (note of the management table). The Qvidja 2021 broiler litter
  is not in the input of Heimsch et al. <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="p. 6, Table 1" />; it is possible that it was
  not applied in the flux footprint.
- **Time stamps.** The raw stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as UTC+2 (findings
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a> and
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>; status: waits for provider) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
  The annual NEE changes only by the 2 hours at the ends of the year; we expect a small effect, but the script does not
  give its size. The management terms do not change.
- **Qvidja and Viikki NEE.** The Qvidja annual NEE of the database is more negative than in Heimsch et al. 2024
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-11`}>QVI-11</a>; status: open question; the annual sums differ by 25 to
  109 g C m⁻² yr⁻¹), so the Qvidja NBE can be too low. The Viikki 2024 gap-filled NEE differs from the measured NEE in some half hours
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-05`}>VII-05</a>; status: known, verified; the annual NEE is 20.4 instead of
  22.5 g C m⁻² yr⁻¹, a difference of 2) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
  The Viikki NEE unit comes from the file headers; a wrong unit would scale NEE but not the management terms.
- **Forest footprint.** The removal is a plot mean for the 200 m radius; the flux footprint of the tower is not the same
  area. The forest NEE comes from another gap-filling and partitioning method than the fields <Cite ids={['kulmala2019']} />.
- **No CH₄.** NBE contains CO₂-C only. The calculated NECB of this project also subtracts CH₄-C
  <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />.
- **Not checked.** No independent recompute or review was made yet. The snapshot is not yet published as a release asset.
