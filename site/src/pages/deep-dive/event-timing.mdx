## Short answer

This dive is a routine inspection of the management events of the six sites. It puts each recorded event on the daily
GPP, Reco and gap-filled NEE of the site, and it finds the day on which the flux changes most near the recorded date. The
crop calendar of the textbook *Peltokasvien tuotanto* <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} /> is the reference for each site.
GPP and Reco are in {U}. NEE below zero is uptake.

- **Harvests.** <Num k="byKind.harvest.n" digits={0} /> of <Num k="byKind.harvest.nSearched" digits={0} /> searched harvest
  episodes have a clear drop of daily GPP. The mean offset from the recorded date is <Num k="byKind.harvest.mean" sign digits={1} unit="d" />
  (SD <Num k="byKind.harvest.sd" digits={1} />, <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval
  <Num k="byKind.harvest.ciLo" sign digits={1} /> to <Num k="byKind.harvest.ciHi" sign digits={1} /> d, median
  <Num k="byKind.harvest.median" digits={0} /> d). The interval excludes zero: on average, the drop comes on or a little
  before the recorded date. In the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days after a harvest, NEE was <Num k="changes.harvest.nee.mean" sign unit={U} /> higher
  than in the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before (n = <Num k="changes.harvest.nee.n" digits={0} />, SD <Num k="changes.harvest.nee.sd" />,
  interval <Num k="changes.harvest.nee.ciLo" sign /> to <Num k="changes.harvest.nee.ciHi" sign />).
- **Other kinds.** Grazing has a mean offset of <Num k="byKind.grazing.mean" sign digits={1} unit="d" />
  (n = <Num k="byKind.grazing.n" digits={0} />, SD <Num k="byKind.grazing.sd" digits={1} />, interval
  <Num k="byKind.grazing.ciLo" sign digits={1} /> to <Num k="byKind.grazing.ciHi" sign digits={1} /> d), and tillage
  <Num k="byKind.tillage.mean" sign digits={1} unit="d" /> (n = <Num k="byKind.tillage.n" digits={0} />,
  SD <Num k="byKind.tillage.sd" digits={1} />, interval <Num k="byKind.tillage.ciLo" sign digits={1} /> to
  <Num k="byKind.tillage.ciHi" sign digits={1} /> d). Both intervals include zero. After a sowing, the largest GPP rise came
  <Num k="tests.sowingRise.mean" digits={1} unit="d" /> later on average (n = <Num k="tests.sowingRise.n" digits={0} />,
  SD <Num k="tests.sowingRise.sd" digits={1} />, interval <Num k="tests.sowingRise.ciLo" digits={1} /> to
  <Num k="tests.sowingRise.ciHi" digits={1} /> d). The
  cleaning cuts gave <Num k="byKind.cut.nClear" digits={0} /> clear drops in <Num k="byKind.cut.nSearched" digits={0} /> tests.
- **Second dates in the sources.** <Num k="counts.nAltDates" digits={0} /> events have a second date in another source.
  <Num k="counts.nAltDatesClear" digits={0} /> of them have a clear change point. The change point is nearer to the date in
  the database for <Num k="counts.nAltNearerUsed" digits={0} />, at the same distance from both dates for
  <Num k="counts.nAltNearerEqual" digits={0} />, and nearer to the other date for <Num k="counts.nAltNearerOther" digits={0} />.

The changes are associated with the events. The weather and the season also change on the same days. The data do not record the crop stage.

**Events.** The events come from `db/events.parquet`: <Num k="counts.events" digits={0} /> events of the six sites (Anttila,
Särkisuo, Haltiala, Qvidja, Viikki and Maktau, in the order of `db/sites.parquet`). The <a href={`${base}events/`}>Events page</a>
gives the source of each event. The event table is typed from the management files of Haltiala, Qvidja and Viikki
<Cite ids={['haltiala-management', 'qvidja-management', 'viikki-management']} />, from the project event file and from the
articles. The build keeps one date for each event.

**Rules.** The script applies these rules in this order.

1. **Local day.** Local day = period start in UTC (`ts_start`) plus the UTC offset of the site (`utc_offset_min`). The daily
   air temperature and soil water content are the means of the 30-min values of `db/observations.parquet`. The daily
   precipitation is the sum; it is empty when all half hours of the day are empty
   (<Num k="dataVersion.steps.observations.precipNullAllHalfHoursNull" digits={0} /> site days). The daily GPP, Reco and
   gap-filled NEE ({U}), the measured fraction of NEE and the daily mean air temperature for the season come from
   `data/calculated/daily.parquet`. Days with a measured fraction below <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % are drawn
   lighter. They are not removed. The timeline of a site is the period from the first to the last day with a gap-filled NEE
   value.
2. **Episodes.** The events of one site form one episode when an event starts not more than
   <Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days after the last day of the current episode (its `date_end`, else its `date`).
   The event "EC system installed" (Haltiala) is not a field operation and is not in an episode. The episode starts on its
   first date and ends on its last `date_end` (or `date`). The *episode kind* is the first type of this list that is in the
   episode: harvest, cut, grazing, tillage, sowing, fertilisation, spraying, other. The *anchor* is the first date of an
   event of the episode kind. This gives <Num k="counts.episodes" digits={0} /> episodes.
3. **Before and after.** The before window is the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before the start (days
   <Num k="method.beforeDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.beforeDays.1" sign digits={0} />). The after window is the
   <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days after the end (days <Num k="method.afterDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to
   <Num k="method.afterDays.1" sign digits={0} />). The change is the mean after minus the mean before of the daily GPP, Reco and
   NEE ({U}). The GPP change is also given in percent of the GPP before, only when the GPP before is
   <Num k="method.minBefore" digits={0} unit={U} /> or more. Each side needs <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with
   a value. An episode with a day of another episode in its windows is flagged "overlap" and is not in the summaries
   (<Num k="counts.overlap" digits={0} /> episodes). The 7-day change uses no other exclusion. A missing day is an empty
   value; the timeline of a site has one row for each calendar day from the first to the last day with a gap-filled NEE.
4. **Change point.** The test is a port of `detectDrop` of `scripts/lib/deep-dive/change-point.ts`. For each candidate day
   *t*, step(*t*) = sign × (mean of the days *t* − <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> to *t* − 1 − mean of the days *t*
   to *t* + <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> − 1). The sign is +1 for a drop and −1 for a rise. Each side needs
   <Num k="method.minSide" digits={0} /> or more values. The detected day is the *t* with the largest step (the first one on a
   tie). The step is *clear* when the higher side (the side before for a drop, the side after for a rise) has a mean of
   <Num k="method.minBefore" digits={0} unit={U} /> or more and the step is <Num k="method.minStepPct" digits={0} /> % or more
   of that mean.
   - Harvest, cut, grazing and tillage episodes: the largest GPP drop, for *t* from the start
     <Num k="method.searchFrom" sign digits={0} /> days to the end <Num k="method.searchTo" sign digits={0} /> days.
   - Episodes with a sowing: the largest GPP rise, for *t* from the sowing date to the sowing date
     <Num k="method.sowingSearchTo" sign digits={0} /> days. The span is long so that it covers the emergence and the early growth.
     The offset of this test is counted from the sowing date (the first sowing event of the episode).
   - Episodes with a tillage: also the largest Reco rise, from the start <Num k="method.searchFrom" sign digits={0} /> days to
     the end <Num k="method.searchTo" sign digits={0} /> days.
   - Fertilisation, spraying and other episodes: no change point. They have only the 7-day change.

   Offset = detected day − anchor, in days. A negative offset means that the flux changed before the recorded date. An
   episode is not in the offset summaries when another episode with a change-point test (harvest, cut, grazing, tillage or
   sowing) has a day in the days that its search reads, from the start <Num k="method.spanOverlapFrom" sign digits={0} />
   days to the end <Num k="method.spanOverlapTo" sign digits={0} /> days. The flag marks <Num k="counts.spanOverlap" digits={0} />
   episodes; {spanOverlapTested} of them has a change-point test (a sowing), the others are fertilisation or spraying
   episodes without a test. This exclusion applies to the summaries per kind and per site and kind. The test rows "all
   episodes with a sowing" and "all tillage episodes, Reco rise" in section "Sites compared" do not apply it.
   **Check on measured NEE:** the daily mean of the measured NEE (`nee` not empty and `nee_qc` 0 or empty) of the half hours
   that start from <Num k="method.dayTimeFromHour" digits={0} />:00 and before <Num k="method.dayTimeToHour" digits={0} />:00
   local time, on days with <Num k="method.minMeasuredHalfHours" digits={0} /> or more such half hours. The script finds the
   largest drop of −NEE in the same span as the GPP drop, with <Num k="method.minSideNee" digits={0} /> or more values per
   side and a higher side of <Num k="method.minBeforeNee" digits={0} unit="µmol m⁻² s⁻¹" /> or more. The two change points
   *agree* when they differ by <Num k="method.agreeDays" digits={0} /> day or less.
5. **Second dates.** Some events have a second date in another source. These dates are not in the database. The script has
   them as one typed table, `ALTERNATIVE_DATES`, with the source and the page or file of each date. For each row, the
   candidate days are the days from <Num k="method.searchFrom" sign digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.searchTo" sign digits={0} />
   days around the date in the database (to its end day) and around the other date (the other date gets the same number
   of days as the event in the database, from `date` to `date_end`). The union of the two spans is searched once; on a tie
   the first day wins. A candidate day is skipped when its side days (*t* − <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> to
   *t* + <Num k="method.sideDays" digits={0} /> − 1) touch a day of another episode of the site (the days of the event itself
   do not skip a candidate). The test is the GPP drop. The result gives the
   offset to each date and the date that is nearer to the detected day. Only a clear change point gives a "nearer" date. For a
   tillage with a GPP drop that is not clear, a clear Reco rise is used. Types without a change-point test
   (fertilisation, spraying) get no result.
6. **Summaries.** Per site and kind, and per kind over all sites: n, mean, SD, median and range of the offsets of the clear
   change points; the counts before (offset below zero), on (zero) and after (above zero) the anchor; and the share of clear
   tests. The <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval of the mean is a percentile bootstrap
   (<Num k="method.bootstrapResamples" digits={0} /> resamples with replacement, the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles of the
   resampled means, `numpy` PCG64, fixed seeds in the manifest) when n is 3 or more; else the text gives the range. The SD
   is the sample SD (n − 1). The median of an even n is the mean of the two middle values. The 7-day changes have the same
   summaries.
7. **Thermal growing season** (Finnish sites). The +<Num k="method.seasonThresholdC" digits={0} /> °C rule comes from the
   book <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('thermalSeason')} />; the run of
   <Num k="method.seasonRunDays" digits={0} /> days is our rule. Start = the first day from the first day of March on with
   a daily mean above +<Num k="method.seasonThresholdC" digits={0} /> °C on that day and on the days after it, together
   <Num k="method.seasonRunDays" digits={0} /> days, all with a value. End = the first day *t* from the first day of August
   on with a daily mean below +<Num k="method.seasonThresholdC" digits={0} /> °C on *t* and the days after it, together
   <Num k="method.seasonRunDays" digits={0} /> days. The length is end minus start, in days. A year has no start when its data begin after
   the first day of March. Maktau has no thermal season: its figure marks the months with a precipitation sum above
   <Num k="method.wetMonthMm" digits={0} unit="mm" /> (our rule).
8. **Counts.** Each step that removes rows or changes values logs a count in `dataVersion.steps` of `results.json` and in
   the manifest.
9. **Open findings.** The script prints the open findings of the data report that touch this dive. Section "Limits and
   uncertainty" gives their effect.

**What a season looks like.** The book gives the crop calendar of the crops of these sites. It gives nothing on GPP, Reco,
NEE or on the days from an event to a flux change. We use it only for the calendar.

- **Silage ley** (Anttila, Särkisuo, Qvidja, Viikki 2022 and 2024). Two or three forage yields are harvested from a silage
  ley per season. The first is usually the largest, and the regrowth stand develops more slowly
  <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('silageCuts')} />. Forage grass is not harvested ripe; the harvest date
  follows the yield and quality targets <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('grassHarvestTiming')} />. A cut
  removes the whole photosynthesising leaf area <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('cutRemovesLeafArea')} />.
  Timothy regrows slowly, from the buds at the base of the shoots
  <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('timothyRegrowth')} />. A dry spell after the first harvest can slow or
  even prevent the regrowth <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('drySpellRegrowth')} />. For a silage ley that
  is harvested three times on coarse mineral soils, the book recommends <Num k="literatureByKey.leyNitrogen.value" digits={0} unit="kg N/ha" />
  <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('leyNitrogen')} />. Leys are established in spring under a cover crop or
  in summer or autumn without one <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('leyEstablishment')} />, and they are
  renewed usually every <Num k="literatureByKey.leyRenewal.valueMin" digits={0} /> to
  <Num k="literatureByKey.leyRenewal.valueMax" digits={0} /> years <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('leyRenewal')} />.
- **Spring cereal** (Haltiala barley and oats, Viikki barley). Spring cereals are sown in April and May and harvested in late
  summer <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('springCerealCalendar')} />. They usually get their fertiliser in one
  application at sowing <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('springCerealFertilisation')} />. The earliest
  varieties start to ripen at the end of July, late varieties only in September; at ripening the leaves die and dry
  <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('cerealRipening')} />. A barley variety needs
  <Num k="literatureByKey.barleyGrowingTime.valueMin" digits={0} /> to <Num k="literatureByKey.barleyGrowingTime.valueMax" digits={0} />
  days, by growing zone <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('barleyGrowingTime')} />.
- **Fava bean** (Viikki 2025). The growing time is <Num k="literatureByKey.favaBeanGrowingTime.value" digits={0} unit="days" />

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as UTC+2
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>; status: waits for provider). A local day of these sites thus starts
  <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours after true local midnight. The day-time window of the measured NEE is
  <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours later in true local time. A day has
  <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours; the shift moves only the first hours of a day. A daily change point
  can thus move by not more than one day.
- **Gap filling.** GPP and Reco are model values from the gap-filled NEE <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. Many days have a
  low measured fraction: Qvidja <Num k="sites.qvidja.daysLowFrac" digits={0} /> of <Num k="sites.qvidja.daysWithNee" digits={0} />,
  Viikki <Num k="sites.viikki.daysLowFrac" digits={0} /> of <Num k="sites.viikki.daysWithNee" digits={0} /> and Maktau
  <Num k="sites.maktau.daysLowFrac" digits={0} /> of <Num k="sites.maktau.daysWithNee" digits={0} /> days. A gap-filled day
  near an event can move or smooth a step. At Viikki, the 2024 gap-filled NEE differs from the measured NEE in many periods
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-05`}>VII-05</a>; status: waits for provider).
- **Small n.** Many site and kind cells have fewer than three clear change points; the text gives the range for them.
  Intervals that rest on fewer than about ten values are not reliable.
- **Search edges.** A detected day at the first day of the search (offset <Num k="method.searchFrom" sign digits={0} unit="d" />)
  can mean that the largest step lies before the span. This occurs for the oats harvest of Haltiala in 2022, the harvest of
  Qvidja on 20 June 2023 and the harvest of Maktau in 2020. For the Haltiala oats, the low GPP before the harvest may reflect
  the ripening of the crop, which the book puts from the end of July on <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={book('cerealRipening')} />;
  the event table has no record of the crop stage.
  The sunflower rise of Haltiala in 2024 is at the end of the sowing search.
- **Events before the flux data.** <Num k="counts.outsideFluxPeriod" digits={0} /> episodes (the sowings of Anttila and
  Särkisuo and the first harvest of Anttila in 2021) lie before the flux data. They are listed, not tested.
- **Season rule.** The run of <Num k="method.seasonRunDays" digits={0} /> days is our rule, not the rule of the book. In warm
  autumns it gives a late end, for example in December 2024 and 2025. At Qvidja in 2025 no run ends the season before the end
  of the year.
- **Book.** The book gives the crop calendar. It does not give the days to emergence of cereals
  or grasses, the calendar dates of cuts, the regrowth days, the timing of grazing, the calendar of sunflower, maize in Kenya,
  or anything on GPP, Reco, NEE or eddy covariance. We do not cite it for these.
- **Sources.** For <Num k="counts.nAltDates" digits={0} /> events the sources give a second date. The database keeps one date
  per event. The tables of each site list the second dates.
- **Weather and meteorology gaps.** The Qvidja rain gauge has a gap from 29 December 2021 to 24 May 2022
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-08`}>QVI-08</a>; status: waits for provider). All Qvidja meteorology is lost from
  11 February to 1 March 2023 (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-09`}>QVI-09</a>; status: resolved); no event is in this
  period. Haltiala has no precipitation in the database. The Maktau findings
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-01`}>MKT-01</a> (status: resolved),
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-02`}>MKT-02</a> (status: open) and
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-03`}>MKT-03</a> (status: waits for provider) are outside the event years 2019 and
  2020 <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
- **Maktau.** The gap-filled values of Maktau come from the MDS gap filling of this project, not from the provider. Maktau has
  no NEE quality flag; all its NEE values count as measured in the NEE check.
- **Association.** The changes and the change points are *associated with* the events. The weather, the season and the crop
  stage also change in the same days. The analysis does not show that an event caused a change.
- **Snapshot.** The database snapshot of this dive is not yet published as a release asset. The hashes in "Reproduce"
  identify it.
