## Short answer

Figure 1 plots annual ecosystem respiration (Reco) against annual gross primary production (GPP). Below the 1:1 line, a
site-year was a net CO₂ sink. The grassland site-years lie near the line. At Haltiala, the oat year 2022 was a sink and
the failed grass year 2023 was a source. NEE does not include the harvest carbon, and n is small.

## Data and method

**Snapshot and script.** The script `analysis/carbon-space/carbon-space.py` reads the harmonised database snapshot built
on {d.dataVersion.snapshot.builtAt}. It reads `db/sites.parquet` and `db/events.parquet`. It also reads the calculated
products of the same build, `data/calculated/annual.csv` and `data/calculated/daily.parquet` <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />.
It checks the SHA-256 and the row count of each file before it reads data (section "Reproduce"). GPP and Reco come from
the night-time partitioning of the calculated products. The figures label Reco as TER (total ecosystem respiration, the
same quantity). NEE is the gap-filled NEE. At Maktau, GPP, Reco and NEE come from
the u* filter and the MDS gap filling of the calculated products <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. All sums use local
calendar years and local days.

**Inclusion.** A site-year is one site and one calendar year. All site-years of the snapshot are candidates. The figures
show site-years from 2019 to 2025. A site-year is in the figures when the annual table marks it as a full year and as
reliable. Reliable means a measured fraction of the half hours of <Num k="method.minFrac" digits={1} /> or more. It also
means a longest gap of <Num k="method.maxGapDays" digits={0} /> days or less <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. Figure 1
also needs an annual GPP and Reco sum. A sum is empty when one half hour has no value. The script gives the reason for
each excluded site-year from these flags (table "Site-years not shown"). The reasons have an order. "No flux data"
(measured fraction 0) comes first. Then come "partial year", "measured fraction" and "longest gap"; a site-year can have
more than one of these. "No annual GPP or Reco sum" applies only to a site-year that is otherwise full and reliable.
Figure 1 has <Num k="summary.nFig1" digits={0} /> site-years. Figures 2 and 3 have <Num k="summary.nFig2" digits={0} />.
Figures 4 and 5 add the <Num k="groups.forest.nee.n" digits={0} /> forest years (paragraph "Reference site").

**Uptake days.** A day is a row of `daily.parquet` with a local date. An uptake day is a day with a daily NEE below zero.
The rule is strict: a daily NEE of exactly zero is not an uptake day. The share is the uptake days divided by the days
with a daily NEE value. Gap-filled days count in full. There is no weighting by the measured fraction and no rule on
the coverage of a day. June to August are the local calendar months 6, 7 and 8 (92 days), also at Maktau.

**Peak window (Figure 3).** June to August is a fixed window. It does not follow the growing season of each site, and
at Maktau it is not the main season. Figure 3 therefore uses a second window: the <Num k="method.peakDays" digits={0} />
consecutive local days with the largest GPP sum of the site-year. The window stays inside the calendar year; it cannot
cross the year end. A window is valid when <Num k="method.peakMinGppDays" digits={0} /> or more of its days have a daily
GPP value. The GPP sum uses the days with a value. When two windows have the same sum, the first window wins. The share
is the uptake days divided by the days with a daily NEE value in the window.

**Land use and events.** Each site-year has a crop and a group, grassland or annual crop. The script constant `CROPS`
gives them, from the project notes. The annual-crop group is {annualCropList}. All other site-years are grassland. This
includes Haltiala 2023 (sown grass that failed) and Viikki 2024. The event counts per type are the events of
`db/events.parquet` in the calendar year of the event start date. "Harvest or cut" is the sum of the harvest and cut
events. "Other" is the sum of the spraying and other events. The events come from the management files
<Cite ids={['haltiala-management', 'qvidja-management', 'viikki-management']} /> and the other sources of the database.

**Reference site (Figures 4 and 5).** The SMEAR II Hyytiälä forest is a reference site with rare management. It is not
in the database. The script reads the years 2019 to 2024 from the SMEAR API: the gap-filled NEE and GPP of table
HYY_EDDY233 and the measured CO₂ flux at 27 m, F_c_270 <Cite ids={['smear-hyytiala']} />. The SMEAR variable metadata
names Kulmala et al. 2019 as the method of the gap filling and the partitioning <Cite ids={['kulmala2019']} />. NEE is
positive for an emission, and GPP is positive. Reco is NEE + GPP. The time base is UTC+2 all year, and a local day is a
calendar day. The daily and annual sums use the same unit conversion as the calculated products. The measured fraction
is the share of the half hours with a measured F_c_270 value. The longest gap is the longest run without it. The
inclusion rule and the uptake windows are the same as for the other sites. The forest is not in the grassland or
annual-crop group. It has its own group, forest. The stand was thinned at the start of
<Num k="literatureByKey.thinningYear.value" digits={0} />: the thinning removed
<Num k="literatureByKey.basalAreaReductionPct.value" digits={0} /> % of the basal area on
<Num k="literatureByKey.thinnedAreaHa.value" digits={0} /> ha (p. 2, Table 1; p. 3, Fig. 1) <Cite ids={['dequesada2025']} />.
Figure 4 labels this year "(thinning)" and the one year before it "(pre-thinning)". Figure 4 has <Num k="summary.fig4.n" digits={0} /> site-years and
Figure 5 has <Num k="summary.nFig5" digits={0} />.

**Line and groups.** The script fits an OLS line, Reco = slope × GPP + intercept, to the Figure 1 site-years. It fits
with and without Maktau. It also gives the Pearson r. The groups give n, mean, sample SD (n − 1), minimum and maximum.
The NEE and Reco/GPP groups use the Figure 1 site-years. The uptake-share groups use the Figure 2 site-years, with
Särkisuo 2023. There is no test. The n is small, and the site-years of one site are not independent.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **NEE is not the full carbon balance.** The harvest takes carbon from the field, and manure brings carbon in. NEE does
  not include these flows. The net ecosystem carbon balance (NECB) with the harvest carbon is on the
  <a href={`${base}calculated/`}>Calculated page</a> <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. Shurpali et al. 2025 add the carbon
  of the harvested biomass to the annual CO₂ flux of 2022. With it, all three sites of that study (Anttila, Särkisuo and
  Pappilansuo) were annual CO₂ sources <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="Table 4, PDF p. 8; PDF p. 9" />. A grassland year
  below the 1:1 line can therefore be a carbon loss of the field.
- **Most half hours are gap-filled at Qvidja and Viikki.** The measured fraction of their site-years in the figures is
  <Num k={`${S('viikki-2025')}.fracMeasured`} /> to <Num k={`${S('qvidja-2020')}.fracMeasured`} />. The annual sums and
  the daily signs depend on the gap-filling model. The effect is not quantified.
- **Qvidja partitioning.** The Qvidja GPP and Reco on this page come from our night-time partitioning. The provider used
  a daytime method. In the provider data, NEE is not equal to Reco − GPP in 26 to 48 % of the periods (finding
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-07`}>QVI-07</a>; status: resolved) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />. Our
  Qvidja GPP and Reco are 15 to 31 % below the provider values <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. The {nQvidjaFig1} Qvidja
  site-years of Figure 1 depend on this choice.
- **Time stamps.** The raw stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as UTC+2 (findings
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a> and
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>; status: waits for provider) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
  A local day of these sites therefore starts 2 hours late (ANT-01). We expect that the annual sums change only by the
  hours at the ends of the year; this is not tested. A daily NEE near zero can change sign. The uptake share can then
  change. The script does not give the size.
- **Qvidja NEE.** The Qvidja annual NEE of the database is more negative than in Heimsch et al. 2024 (open question,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-11`}>QVI-11</a>; Data Report status: waits for provider) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
  The Qvidja site-years can lie too far below the 1:1 line. Viikki 2024 gap-filled NEE differs from the measured NEE in
  some half hours (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-05`}>VII-05</a>; status: waits for provider).
- **Maktau has one year.** Only Maktau 2019 passes the inclusion rule. In Maktau, the long rains are from March to May
  and the short rains from October to December <Cite ids={['rasanen2026']} note="PDF p. 2" />. In 2019 the maize was
  sown on 12 April and harvested on 13 August <Cite ids={['rasanen2026']} note="PDF p. 3" />. June to August is not the
  main growing season of Maktau. Its June-to-August share does not compare directly with the Finnish sites.
- **Few events.** A site-year has few management events, and each site has a different set of years. The dive
  therefore makes no statistical test of the events. The links between the events and the position of a site-year are
  descriptive. The event table can be incomplete for a site-year.
- **Forest data from a different pipeline.** The forest NEE and GPP come from the SMEAR gap filling and partitioning
  <Cite ids={['kulmala2019']} />. The fields use the night-time partitioning of this project <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />.
  The GPP and Reco of the forest and of the fields are therefore not fully comparable.
- **Forest source year.** Hyytiälä 2020 is a net source in the SMEAR data as delivered <Cite ids={['smear-hyytiala']} />.
  The stand was thinned at the start of 2020 <Cite ids={['dequesada2025']} />, and the source year agrees with this. The
  set has one year before the thinning (2019) and four after it, so the dive does not separate the effect of the
  thinning from the effect of the weather of 2020. The wood removed in the thinning is not in NEE.
- **Figures 3, 4 and 5 after the checks.** Figures 3, 4 and 5 were added after the independent checks. Their numbers
  are not independently recomputed.
- **Snapshot.** The database snapshot of this dive is not yet published as a release asset. The hashes in "Reproduce"
  identify it.
