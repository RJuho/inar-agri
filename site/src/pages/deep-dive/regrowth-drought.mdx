## Short answer

This dive compares the regrowth of four grasslands after a silage harvest. A harvest in a dry month is compared with
a harvest in a normal month. The measure is the light use efficiency LUE = GPP / PAR per local day ({ULUE}), as a fraction
of the LUE of the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before the harvest. GPP is the daily GPP of the
night-time partitioning <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. PAR is the daily photosynthetic photon flux ({UPAR}). A dry month
has SPI-1 ≤ <Num k="method.spiDry" digits={1} /> at the FMI station of the site <Cite ids={['spi-data']} />. The comparison
stays within the same cut number: first cuts against first cuts, later cuts against later cuts. Day 0 is the last day of
the harvest. All values below are fractions of the LUE, GPP or PAR before the harvest.

With these data, a dry harvest month gives no clear LUE penalty for the regrowth. The intervals are wide, and the dry

## Data and method

**Snapshot and script.** The script `analysis/regrowth-drought/regrowth-drought.py` reads the harmonised database snapshot
built on {d.dataVersion.snapshot.builtAt} (`db/events.parquet`, `db/sites.parquet`, `db/variables.parquet` and the
columns `ppfd`, `sw_in` and `swc` of `db/observations.parquet`), `data/calculated/daily.parquet` of the same build
<Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} /> and the SPI table `data/SPI/SPI_all_stations.csv` <Cite ids={['spi-data']} />. It
checks the SHA-256 and the row count of each file before it reads data (section "Reproduce"). The episodes and the
windows are those of Deep Dive #7 (<a href={`${base}deep-dive/silage-harvest-response/`}>Silage harvest and grassland
CO₂ fluxes</a>). All windows include both end days. The nine rules below give each constant; open them to reproduce the
numbers.

<details class="dd-table" id="method-rules">
<summary>Rules of the analysis (nine rules and the overrides)</summary>

1. **Data and local day.** The daily GPP (g C m⁻² d⁻¹), TER, gap-filled NEE and the measured fraction of NEE per local
   day come from `data/calculated/daily.parquet`. GPP and TER are model values of the night-time partitioning
   <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. The 30-minute `ppfd`, `sw_in` and `swc` of the four sites come from
   `db/observations.parquet`. The local day of a 30-minute row is the period start in UTC plus the UTC offset of the site
   (`utc_offset_min`, UTC+2 for all four sites). A day counts when <Num k="method.minHalfHours" digits={0} /> or more of
   its <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours have a value. The mean of the values that exist fills the
   gap. The events come from `db/events.parquet`. Day 0 is the last day of the harvest (*end*).
2. **Episodes.** The episodes are those of Deep Dive #7. The events of `type` `harvest` and `cut` of the five Finnish
   sites are sorted by date. An event spans the days `date` to `date_end`, or `date` only. An event that starts not more
   than <Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days after the last day of the current episode joins it. Then the
   <Num k="counts.overrides" digits={0} /> overrides of Deep Dive #4 apply (list below). A `harvest` is a grain harvest
   when its `crop` or `detail` matches one of the words thresh, grain, faba, oats or barley (the regular expression
   `thresh|grain|faba|\boats\b|barley`, case-insensitive) and does not match cut, silage or grass
   (`\bcut\b|silage|grass`). A grain harvest is not an episode of this dive. Thus the Haltiala barley and oats harvests
   and the Viikki faba bean harvest of 10 September 2025 are not episodes. A `cut` is a cleaning cut. The dive keeps the
   <Num k="counts.silageEpisodes" digits={0} /> silage harvest episodes. The *cut number* of an episode is its ordinal
   among the harvest and cut episodes of the site in that year, cleaning cuts included. The year of an episode is the
   year of *start*. *First cut* = cut number 1 (the Viikki cut of 1 July 2022 is a first cut); *later cuts* = cut number
   2 or more.
3. **Windows and exclusions.** The windows count from the first day (*start*) and the last day (*end*) of the episode.
   Before = start <Num k="method.beforeDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to start <Num k="method.beforeDays.1" sign digits={0} />.
   After = end <Num k="method.afterDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to end <Num k="method.afterDays.1" sign digits={0} />.
   Late = end <Num k="method.lateDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to end <Num k="method.lateDays.1" sign digits={0} />. The
   exclusions of Deep Dive #7 apply in this order. (1) *Overlap*: another harvest or cut episode of the site, or a
   grazing or tillage event, has a day in common with the days start <Num k="method.beforeDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to
   end <Num k="method.afterDays.1" sign digits={0} /> (<Num k="counts.excluded.overlap" digits={0} /> episodes). (2) *Too
   few flux days*: GPP, TER or NEE has fewer than <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with a value in the before or
   the after window (<Num k="counts.excluded.too-few-days" digits={0} />). A day with a value is a day with a non-null
   daily `gpp_gc`, `reco_gc` (TER) or `nee_f_gc` (NEE) in `daily.parquet`. (3) *Low measured fraction*: the mean measured
   fraction of NEE is below <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % in the before or the after window
   (<Num k="counts.excluded.low-measured-fraction" digits={0} />). This mean is the plain mean of `frac_measured` over
   the days of the window with a non-null value. A window without such a day counts as below the limit.
   <Num k="counts.usedFlux" digits={0} /> episodes pass these rules, the same as in Deep Dive #7. The late window has the
   same overlap test as (1), applied to the days end <Num k="method.lateDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to end
   <Num k="method.lateDays.1" sign digits={0} />. When another harvest or cut episode, or a grazing or tillage event, has
   a day in common with them, the late window is empty. A fertilisation in the days start
   <Num k="method.beforeDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to end <Num k="method.afterDays.1" sign digits={0} /> only sets a flag. Two Viikki episodes are *flagged*: 17 August 2023
   (establishment year: barley undersown with clover and grasses, cut green on two days) and 14 June 2024 (partial harvest
   of about one hectare). They are not a regrowth of an established grass stand after a full harvest. They stay in Table A
   and in Figure 3 with open markers, but they are not in the groups and not in the curves of Figures 1 and 2
   (<Num k="counts.flagged" digits={0} /> episodes).
4. **Daily PAR and global radiation.** Daily PAR ({UPAR}) = the mean of the 30-minute `ppfd` (µmol m⁻² s⁻¹) of the
   local day × <Num k="method.dayFactor" digits={4} /> (86 400 s × 10⁻⁶). Daily global radiation SW_in (MJ m⁻² d⁻¹) = the
   mean `sw_in` (W m⁻²) × the same factor. Both use the day rule of rule 1.
5. **Qvidja PAR from global radiation.** Qvidja has no `ppfd`. Its daily PAR = SW_in × k. k (mol MJ⁻¹) is the pooled
   median of the daily ratio PAR / SW_in of the sites with both columns, over the days with PAR of
   <Num k="method.minPar" digits={0} /> {UPAR} or more: Anttila and Viikki always, and Särkisuo because it has
   <Num k="method.kMinDays" digits={0} /> or more such days. k = <Num k="par.k" digits={3} /> (<Num k="par.pooled.nDays" digits={0} />
   days; 10 to 90 % range <Num k="par.pooled.p10" digits={3} /> to <Num k="par.pooled.p90" digits={3} />). The per-site
   medians are <Num k="par.perSite.anttila.median" digits={3} /> (Anttila), <Num k="par.perSite.sarkisuo.median" digits={3} />
   (Särkisuo) and <Num k="par.perSite.viikki.median" digits={3} /> (Viikki); Table D gives the ranges. The energy fraction
   of PAR in the global radiation is k / <Num k="method.parUmolPerJ" digits={2} /> = <Num k="par.energyFractionPct" digits={1} /> %.
   The conversion <Num k="method.parUmolPerJ" digits={2} /> µmol J⁻¹ is an assumption of this project. No page is cited
   for it. Heimsch et al. 2024 estimate PAR as <Num k="literatureByKey.parFractionOfGlobal.value" digits={0} /> % of the
   global radiation <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note={L('parFractionOfGlobal')} />. The relative LUE, GPP and PAR
   of a Qvidja episode do not depend on k, because k cancels in value / reference; k only changes which Qvidja days have
   a PAR below <Num k="method.minPar" digits={0} /> {UPAR} and the absolute LUE before the harvest. The runs with the
   smallest and the largest per-site median (<Num k="sensitivity.k.kMin" digits={3} /> and
   <Num k="sensitivity.k.kMax" digits={3} />) gave the same group results.
6. **LUE and the relative values.** LUE of a day = GPP of the day / PAR of the day ({ULUE}). A day with a PAR below
   <Num k="method.minPar" digits={0} /> {UPAR} has no LUE (<Num k="counts.daysParBelowMin" digits={0} /> site days of all
   data). The *reference* of an episode is the days start <Num k="method.beforeDays.0" sign digits={0} /> to start
   <Num k="method.beforeDays.1" sign digits={0} /> with a LUE value: GPP before and PAR before are the means over these
   days, and LUE before = GPP before / PAR before. This is a ratio of means, thus on each day the relative GPP = the
   relative LUE × the relative PAR. An episode with fewer than <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> reference LUE days is
   excluded (*too few LUE days before*, <Num k="counts.excluded.too-few-par-days" digits={0} /> episode: Särkisuo
   15 August 2023, a PPFD gap). The relative LUE, GPP and PAR of a day = the value / the reference. A window mean is the
   mean over the days of the window with a LUE value. The window means of the relative LUE, GPP and PAR use the same
   days. A window mean is empty with fewer than <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> such days. The late window is also
   empty when the overlap test of rule 3 finds an event in it. The product of the window means of the relative LUE and PAR is not the window mean of
   the relative GPP, because a mean of products is not the product of the means; the tables give the window mean of the
   relative GPP. <Num k="counts.used" digits={0} /> episodes have a reference, and <Num k="counts.inGroups" digits={0} />
   of them are in the groups.
7. **Dryness.** The SPI-1 of the FMI station of the site <Cite ids={['spi-data']} /> in the calendar month that holds
   *end*: Vantaa for Viikki, Kaarina Yltöinen for Qvidja, Kuopio Maaninka for Anttila and Särkisuo. *Dry* = SPI-1 ≤
   <Num k="method.spiDry" digits={1} />. This limit is the lower bound of "severely dry" in the SPEI classes that Heimsch
   et al. 2024 use <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note={L('speiSeverelyDry')} />. Deep Dive #3
   (<a href={`${base}deep-dive/nee-without-harvest-drought/`}>NEE without harvest or drought</a>) uses SPI-1 ≤
   <Num k="method.spiDryOtherDive" digits={1} />. June 2018 at Kaarina Yltöinen has SPI-1
   <Num k="summary.extra.june2018.spi1" /> and SPI-3 <Num k="summary.extra.june2018.spi3" />; thus the Qvidja cut of
   12 June 2018 is not dry by the rule of this dive, although Heimsch et al. 2024 classify May, June and July 2018 as
   extremely dry by SPEI <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note={L('qvidja2018Drought')} />. Three sensitivity rules apply to the same <Num k="counts.inGroups" digits={0} /> episodes as the main rule. (1) SPI-3 ≤
   <Num k="method.spiDry" digits={1} /> of the same month. (2) SWC anomaly below zero. The daily `swc` is the mean of the
   30-minute `swc` of the local day, with the day rule of rule 1. The anomaly = the mean daily `swc` of the local days
   end <Num k="method.swcWindow.0" sign digits={0} /> to end <Num k="method.swcWindow.1" sign digits={0} /> minus the mean
   of the same site on the same calendar dates in all its data years. Each data year with at least one daily `swc` in
   these dates counts, the episode year included. The Särkisuo cuts of 2022 have one year with `swc`, thus an anomaly of
   0. (3) SPI-1 ≤ <Num k="sensitivity.spiMinus1.threshold" digits={1} />. The sensitivity rules give group means only.
8. **Groups and statistics.** The statistics are per cut group (first, later), dryness (dry, normal) and window (after,
   late), for the relative LUE, GPP and PAR. Each group gives n, mean, sample SD (n − 1), median and range. A percentile
   bootstrap gives the <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval of the mean when n is
   <Num k="method.bootstrapMinN" digits={0} /> or more (<Num k="method.bootstrapResamples" digits={0} /> resamples with
   replacement, the 2.5 and 97.5 percentiles, `numpy` PCG64 with fixed seeds in the manifest). The difference dry −
   normal has a bootstrap interval that resamples both groups (both n of <Num k="method.bootstrapMinN" digits={0} /> or
   more). An exact two-sided permutation test compares the difference of means over all C(n, n dry) splits of the group.
   p = the share of splits with an absolute difference at or above the observed one. With one dry episode (later cuts)
   there is no test and no interval. The single value is then given against the mean, SD and range of the normal group,
   also in SD units. Composite curves per group cover the days <Num k="method.compositeFrom" sign digits={0} /> to
   <Num k="method.compositeTo" sign digits={0} /> from end. The value of an episode is empty from the day of the next
   harvest, cut or grazing of the site on. Per day the curve gives the mean, SD, n and the bootstrap interval. A line
   needs <Num k="method.compositeMinN" digits={0} /> or more episodes with a value, a band
   <Num k="method.bootstrapMinN" digits={0} /> or more. A group of one episode shows the episode itself. The y range of
   Figures 1 and 2 is one range over all drawn means and bands. It is rounded outwards to a multiple of
   <Num k="method.yStep" digits={2} /> and extended to hold 0 and 1: <Num k="composite.yRange.0" digits={2} /> to
   <Num k="composite.yRange.1" digits={2} />.
9. **Counts and findings.** Each step that removes rows or changes values logs a count in `dataVersion.steps` of
   `results.json`. The script prints the open findings of the data report that touch this dive.

## Results

Shurpali et al. 2025 report that after each harvest all their ecosystems turned into CO₂ sources, until the regrowth and
the increasing GPP exceeded the ecosystem respiration <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="PDF p. 11" />. Deep Dive #7
measured this drop and rise of GPP. This dive looks at the regrowth through the LUE, so that a difference in light after
the harvest is taken out. The textbook states that the speed of the regrowth after a cut is a property of the species and
the cultivar, changed by the cutting height, the number of cuts and the nutrient supply, and that a large leaf area left in
the stubble can speed up the regrowth, especially in dry conditions <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={L('regrowthAfterCut')} />.
It also states that the regrowth yield develops more slowly, and that its feed value does not change as fast as in spring <Cite ids={['peltokasvien-tuotanto']} note={L('laterCutSlower')} />.
Thus the dive compares within the same cut number.

Figures 1 and 2 show the relative LUE per day from <Num k="method.compositeFrom" digits={0} abs /> days before to
<Num k="method.compositeTo" digits={0} /> days after the last day of the harvest. A line is the mean of the episodes of
the group; the band is the bootstrap <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval of the mean. Figure 3 shows the
LUE of the late window of each episode against the SPI-1 of its harvest month.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Site and dryness are confounded.** All <Num k="groups.first.n.dry" digits={0} /> dry first cuts and the
  <Num k="groups.later.n.dry" digits={0} /> dry later cut are at Qvidja (Kaarina Yltöinen station). The normal groups hold
  episodes of {normalFirstSites} (first cuts) and {normalLaterSites} (later cuts). Qvidja differs from the other sites in its location, its data years, its grass stand and its
  PAR source (table "Sites"). A difference between the dry and the normal group can be a difference
  between Qvidja and the other sites. The analysis does not show that drought caused a change.
- **Small n.** The dry first-cut group has n = <Num k="groups.first.n.dry" digits={0} />, the dry later-cut group
  n = <Num k="groups.later.n.dry" digits={0} />. A bootstrap interval of n = <Num k="method.bootstrapMinN" digits={0} />
  is close to the range of the three values. The permutation test has <Num k="groups.first.after.relLue.diff.nSplits" digits={0} />
  (after) and <Num k="groups.first.late.relLue.diff.nSplits" digits={0} /> (late) possible splits; the smallest possible p
  is thus not small. The dashed curve of Figure 2 is one episode and shows no group behaviour.
- **Qvidja PAR from global radiation.** The Qvidja PAR is SW_in × k with one k for all days (rule 5). The ratio PAR /
  SW_in of the other sites varies from day to day (10 to 90 % range <Num k="par.pooled.p10" digits={2} /> to
  <Num k="par.pooled.p90" digits={2} /> mol MJ⁻¹, Table D). The relative values of a Qvidja episode do not depend on k, but
  a day-to-day change of the true ratio is not in the Qvidja PAR. The energy fraction k / <Num k="method.parUmolPerJ" digits={2} />
  = <Num k="par.energyFractionPct" digits={1} /> % rests on an assumed conversion. Heimsch et al. 2024 estimate PAR as
  <Num k="literatureByKey.parFractionOfGlobal.value" digits={0} /> % of the global radiation
  <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note={L('parFractionOfGlobal')} />.
- **SPI is a station value of a calendar month.** The SPI-1 comes from the FMI station of the site, not from the site
  <Cite ids={['spi-data']} />, and it describes the calendar month that holds the last harvest day, not the windows of the
  episode. A harvest early in the month gets the SPI of a month that is mostly after it. The SWC anomaly (Table A) is a
  site value, but its reference has few years at Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki.
- **SPI against SPEI in 2018.** The dry rule of this dive uses SPI-1 ≤ <Num k="method.spiDry" digits={1} />. June 2018 at
  Kaarina Yltöinen has SPI-1 <Num k="summary.extra.june2018.spi1" />, thus the Qvidja cut of 12 June 2018 is a normal
  episode here, although Heimsch et al. 2024 classify May, June and July 2018 as extremely dry by SPEI <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note={L('qvidja2018Drought')} />. With SPI-1 ≤
  <Num k="sensitivity.spiMinus1.threshold" digits={1} />, it is a dry episode (Table C).
- **Model values and negative days.** GPP is a model value of the night-time partitioning of the gap-filled NEE
  <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. On some days the daily GPP is below zero, and the relative LUE of that day is then
  below zero; the y range of Figures 1 and 2 therefore starts at <Num k="composite.yRange.0" digits={2} />. A window
  mean of <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> or more days reduces this effect but does not remove it. A day with a PAR
  below <Num k="method.minPar" digits={0} /> {UPAR} has no LUE.
- **LUE is a ratio.** A low PAR raises the relative LUE at the same GPP. The dry later cut had a relative PAR of
  <Num k="groups.later.after.relPar.dry.mean" /> (after) and <Num k="groups.later.late.relPar.dry.mean" /> (late), below
  the normal means <Num k="groups.later.after.relPar.normal.mean" /> (SD <Num k="groups.later.after.relPar.normal.sd" />,
  range <Num k="groups.later.after.relPar.normal.min" /> to <Num k="groups.later.after.relPar.normal.max" />) and
  <Num k="groups.later.late.relPar.normal.mean" /> (SD <Num k="groups.later.late.relPar.normal.sd" />, range
  <Num k="groups.later.late.relPar.normal.min" /> to <Num k="groups.later.late.relPar.normal.max" />). Its relative LUE
  was above the normal range in the late window. Its relative GPP was <Num k="groups.later.after.relGpp.dry.mean" />
  (after, <Num k="groups.later.after.relGpp.diff.single.sdUnits" digits={1} /> SD units above the normal mean
  <Num k="groups.later.after.relGpp.normal.mean" />, SD <Num k="groups.later.after.relGpp.normal.sd" />, range
  <Num k="groups.later.after.relGpp.normal.min" /> to <Num k="groups.later.after.relGpp.normal.max" />) and
  <Num k="groups.later.late.relGpp.dry.mean" /> (late, <Num k="groups.later.late.relGpp.diff.single.sdUnits" digits={1} />
  SD units above the normal mean <Num k="groups.later.late.relGpp.normal.mean" />, SD
  <Num k="groups.later.late.relGpp.normal.sd" />, range <Num k="groups.later.late.relGpp.normal.min" /> to
  <Num k="groups.later.late.relGpp.normal.max" />). GPP may respond to light in a non-linear way. The ratio GPP / PAR is
  then an approximation.
- **Fertilisation.** {fertCount} of the <Num k="counts.inGroups" digits={0} /> episodes in the groups have a fertilisation
  in the days −<Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> to +<Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> (Table A). The dive
  does not separate its effect.
- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC. The build reads them as UTC+2
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>; status: open, waits for provider) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
  A local day of these sites thus holds the true local time from <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} />:00 on that
  date to <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} />:00 on the next date. The daily PAR and GPP of these sites hold
  <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours of the next true day. The effect is not quantified here. The Qvidja
  stamps are not part of these findings.
- **Viikki 2024.** The gap-filled NEE of Viikki 2024 is not equal to the measured NEE where a measured value exists
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-05`}>VII-05</a>; status: known, verified, waits for provider)
  <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />. The GPP of the partitioning around the 2024 Viikki harvests can thus differ from the
  measured values. Both Viikki 2024 episodes are outside the groups: 14 June is flagged, and 31 July has a low measured
  fraction.
- **Snapshot.** The database snapshot of this dive is not yet published as a release asset. The hashes in "Reproduce"
  identify it.
