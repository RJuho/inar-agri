## Short answer

This dive asks one question from <a href={`${base}deep-dive/disturbance-detail/`}>Deep Dive #4</a>. Is the change of GPP
after a fertilisation larger than the change that the weather of the same days explains? The dive uses the windows and rules
of <a href={`${base}deep-dive/disturbance-size/`}>Deep Dive #1</a>. The analysis is a Python script that reads one fixed
snapshot of the harmonised database. A positive change of GPP means more uptake of CO₂ after the episode.

  <Num k="summary.fertOnly.observed.mean" sign unit={U} /> (SD <Num k="summary.fertOnly.observed.sd" />,
  <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval <Num k="summary.fertOnly.observed.ciLo" sign /> to
  <Num k="summary.fertOnly.observed.ciHi" sign />). In control windows of the same season, the weather change gives an

## Data and method

**Snapshot and script.** The script `analysis/fertilisation-weather/fertilisation-weather.py` reads the harmonised database
snapshot of 2026-10-23 (`db/events.parquet`, `db/sites.parquet`, `db/observations.parquet`, `db/variables.parquet`) and
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. It checks the SHA-256 and the row count of each file
before it reads data. Section "Reproduce" gives the hashes, the row counts and the command.

**Events.** The events come from `db/events.parquet`: <Num k="counts.events" digits={0} /> events of the five Finnish sites
(Anttila, Särkisuo, Haltiala, Qvidja and Viikki). The <a href={`${base}events/`}>Events page</a> lists each event with its
date, amount, nitrogen input and source. Maktau is not in this dive. Each event gets one of four classes: *harvest or cut*,
*grazing*, *fertilisation* or *tillage*; sowing, spraying and other events have no class. A fertilisation event is *mineral*
when its text contains "mineral", "synthetic", "saltpetre", "salpietari", "AXAN", "Typpi" or "CAN" (not case-sensitive;
"mineral", "synthetic", "AXAN", "Typpi" and "CAN" as whole words); all other fertilisation events are *organic*. Thus the
events with the text "product not given" (Anttila and Särkisuo 2023, Qvidja April 2021) count as organic, and the own NPK mix
of Qvidja 2020 counts as organic.

**Episodes.** Class events of a site are one episode when the gap from the end of one event to the start of the next is
not more than <Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days; the episode ends on the last day of its last event. This gives <Num k="counts.classEpisodes" digits={0} /> episodes,
of these <Num k="counts.fertEpisodes" digits={0} /> *fertilisation episodes* (an episode with at least one fertilisation
event): Anttila <Num k="counts.perSite.anttila.fertEpisodes" digits={0} />, Särkisuo <Num k="counts.perSite.sarkisuo.fertEpisodes" digits={0} />,
Haltiala <Num k="counts.perSite.haltiala.fertEpisodes" digits={0} />, Qvidja <Num k="counts.perSite.qvidja.fertEpisodes" digits={0} />,
Viikki <Num k="counts.perSite.viikki.fertEpisodes" digits={0} />. An episode is *with harvest or cut* when it contains a
harvest or cut event, and *with tillage or sowing* when it contains a tillage event or a sowing event of the site falls on one
of its days. The product split (*mineral*, *organic*) is the kind of its fertilisation events; an episode with both kinds
would be in neither split (there is none). The nitrogen input is the sum of the recorded N of these events.

**Daily values.** NEE, GPP and Reco are the daily sums of the gap-filled, partitioned fluxes in {U} from
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />, with the measured fraction of NEE per day. Air temperature
is the daily mean of the same file. From `db/observations.parquet` per local day (period start plus the UTC offset of the
site): the *light* is the mean of the half-hour PPFD, negative values set to zero, times the seconds of a day
(mol m⁻² d⁻¹); at Qvidja, which has no PPFD, it is the incoming short-wave radiation (MJ m⁻² d⁻¹). The mean divides by all
<Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours of the day, also when a half hour has no value, and the day needs at
least <Num k="method.minLightRows" digits={0} /> half hours with a value. The *rain* is the daily sum of the precipitation
(at least <Num k="method.minRainRows" digits={0} /> half hours). Haltiala has no rain sensor; we used the rain of Viikki for
Haltiala. The *soil water* is the daily mean of the volumetric soil water content (at least <Num k="method.minSwcRows" digits={0} />
half hours); it is empty when the daily mean soil temperature is at or below zero. The *light-normalised GPP* is the daily GPP
divided by the daily light, on days with a light of at least <Num k="method.minLueLightPpfd" digits={0} /> mol m⁻² d⁻¹
(PPFD) or <Num k="method.minLueLightSwIn" digits={1} /> MJ m⁻² d⁻¹ (short-wave).

**Windows and rules.** The *before* window is the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before the first day of the
episode. The *after* window is the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days after its last day. A window mean of a
variable uses the days of the window with a value of that variable. The *change* (Δ) is the mean after minus the mean
before; it is empty when a side has fewer than <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with a value. Δlight is given in
percent of the light before. Δrain is the change of the mean daily rain times <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> (mm per
window). The flux result uses an episode only when it passes three rules, checked in this order:

- **Rule 1.** No other class episode of the site (of any of the four classes) *starts* in the before or the after window. An
  episode that started earlier and continues into a window does not exclude it.
- **Rule 2.** Each window has at least <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with a value, for each of the gap-filled
  NEE, GPP and Reco.
- **Rule 3.** The mean of the daily measured fraction of NEE, over the days of the window with a value, is at least
  <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % in each window.

<Num k="counts.fertFluxUsed" digits={0} /> of the <Num k="counts.fertEpisodes" digits={0} /> fertilisation episodes passed
(<Num k="counts.fertExclusions.overlap" digits={0} /> failed rule 1, <Num k="counts.fertExclusions.too-few-days" digits={0} />
rule 2, <Num k="counts.fertExclusions.low-measured-fraction" digits={0} /> rule 3).

**Control pairs.** For each site, a *control pair* is a before and after window pair of the same length around an *anchor
day*. The before window is the <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days before the anchor day and the after window the
<Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} /> days after it; the anchor day itself is in no window. The anchors of a site are all
days, in all years in which the site has daily flux values, whose day of year (plain day count of the year, no leap-year
correction) is within <Num k="method.controlHalfWidth" digits={0} /> days of the day of year of the first day of a
fertilisation episode of the site. An anchor is dropped when a management event of any type (also sowing, spraying and other
events, and the fertilisations themselves) is within <Num k="method.maskBefore" digits={0} /> days of any day of its windows.
A pair is used only when:

- each window has at least <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with NEE, GPP and Reco, with air temperature and with
  light;
- the mean measured fraction of NEE is at least <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> % in each window;
- the light before is above zero.

This gives <Num k="counts.controlPairs" digits={0} /> control pairs: Haltiala <Num k="counts.controlPairsPerSite.haltiala" digits={0} />,
Qvidja <Num k="counts.controlPairsPerSite.qvidja" digits={0} />, Viikki <Num k="counts.controlPairsPerSite.viikki" digits={0} />,
Särkisuo <Num k="counts.controlPairsPerSite.sarkisuo" digits={0} /> and Anttila <Num k="counts.controlPairsPerSite.anttila" digits={0} />.
The flux data of Anttila and Särkisuo cover only 2022 and 2023, and the events of these years leave almost no windows without
management. These two sites have no fit, and their episodes are not in the summaries.

**Weather fit, expected change and residual.** For each site with pairs we fitted ΔGPP = a + b × Δta + c × Δlight by least
squares on its control pairs (the fit needs at least five pairs; its residual SD divides by n − 3). The *expected* ΔGPP of an
episode is this fit at the Δta and Δlight of the episode; it is empty when one of the three values is empty. The *residual*
is the observed minus the expected ΔGPP. The *residual rank* is the percent of the control residuals of the site (the
residuals of the same fit on its own pairs) that are strictly below the residual of the episode; the *raw rank* does the same
with the observed ΔGPP. Two model-free checks:
(a) *matched controls*, the control pairs of the site with |Δta| within <Num k="method.tolTa" digits={0} unit="°C" /> and
|Δlight| within <Num k="method.tolLightPct" digits={0} unit="%" /> of the episode (at least <Num k="method.minMatched" digits={0} /> pairs),
and the difference of the episode to their mean ΔGPP; (b) the change of the light-normalised GPP, in percent. Further checks:
the fit with Δsoil water as a third predictor; anchors within <Num k="method.controlHalfWidthSens" digits={0} /> days instead
of <Num k="method.controlHalfWidth" digits={0} />; and one pooled fit of the relative change of GPP (in percent of the GPP
before, at least <Num k="method.minGppBefore" digits={1} unit={U} />) over the pairs of all sites.

**Summaries.** The summaries use the episodes that passed the three rules, have no harvest or cut, and have a site fit:
<Num k="counts.fertInSummary" digits={0} /> episodes. We split them into *fertilisation only* and *with tillage or sowing*,
and into *mineral* and *organic*. Per split: n, mean, SD (n − 1) and a <Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % percentile
bootstrap interval of the mean (resampling of the episodes of the split, <Num k="method.bootstrapResamples" digits={0} />
resamples, fixed seeds, `numpy` generator; the seeds are in the manifest). An interval needs at least three episodes. With fewer than about ten episodes it is not reliable. The control
pairs overlap in time and are not independent. Thus we give ranks and no p-values for the control fits.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Small n.** The summaries have <Num k="counts.fertInSummary" digits={0} /> episodes; the fertilisation-only split has
  <Num k="summary.fertOnly.n" digits={0} /> and the mineral split <Num k="summary.mineral.n" digits={0} />. The SD of the
  residual is <Num k="summary.all.residual.sd" unit={U} /> over all <Num k="summary.all.n" digits={0} /> episodes. The intervals
  of the small splits are not reliable; the tables give the single episodes.
- **Weather fit.** The fit is linear in Δta and Δlight and does not suit the episodes with tillage or sowing. The residuals of the episodes
  with tillage or sowing do not show a fertiliser effect. Δta and Δlight are correlated in spring:
  r = <Num k="controls.haltiala.rTaLight" /> at Haltiala, <Num k="controls.qvidja.rTaLight" /> at Qvidja and
  <Num k="controls.viikki.rTaLight" /> at Viikki. Above <Num k="method.collinearR" digits={1} /> (a rule of this project, not a
  tested limit) we do not read the single coefficients; the expected value of the fit is less affected than the coefficients.
- **Control pairs.** The anchors step by one day, so the pairs overlap and are not independent. We give ranks and no
  p-values. Anttila has <Num k="counts.controlPairsPerSite.anttila" digits={0} /> and Särkisuo
  <Num k="counts.controlPairsPerSite.sarkisuo" digits={0} /> control pairs. Their episodes are in the episode table only.
- **Product classes.** The mineral rule is a text rule on the event description. Events without a product name count as
  organic. A change of the rule changes the product split, not the per-episode results.
- **Gap filling.** GPP is a model value from the gap-filled NEE <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. Rule 3 removes windows with
  a low measured fraction, but the gap-filled days in the other windows can smooth a change.
- **Rain and soil water.** Haltiala has no rain sensor; we used the Viikki rain as a proxy. The Qvidja rain gauge has no
  data from 29 December 2021 to 24 May 2022 (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-08`}>finding QVI-08</a>). Δrain is thus
  descriptive only. The Haltiala soil water has a coarse resolution with flat periods
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-HAL-01`}>finding HAL-01</a>). The soil water is empty when the daily mean soil
  temperature is at or below zero, because the Qvidja sensor is not reliable in frozen soil
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-16`}>finding QVI-16</a>, open, decision pending) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build adds UTC+2 to them. These
  values are <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours early
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a>,
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a>; open, wait for the providers). On a daily scale this moves
  <Num k="method.utcShiftHalfHours" digits={0} /> of the <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours of a day to
  the next day. A shift of <Num k="method.utcShiftHalfHours" digits={0} /> half hours changes a
  <Num k="method.windowDays" digits={0} />-day mean only through its first and its last day.
- **Light mean.** The daily light mean counts a missing half hour as zero light and divides by all
  <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours. A day with the minimum of <Num k="method.minLightRows" digits={0} />
  values can thus have a light sum that is a little too low. Δlight is in percent and the same rule applies to both windows.
- **Event dates.** The episode dates are the dates of the field work in the management files and the project event file.
  The Events page gives the source of each event. A date error of one or two days moves the windows by the same days.
- **Snapshot.** The database snapshot of this dive is not yet published as a release asset. The hashes in "Reproduce" identify
  it.
- **Association.** The changes are *associated with* the events. The weather, the season and the crop stage also change in
  the same windows.

## Reproduce

The numbers on this page come from `analysis/fertilisation-weather/results.json`. The Python script
[`analysis/fertilisation-weather/fertilisation-weather.py`](https://github.com/RJuho/inar-agri/blob/main/analysis/fertilisation-weather/fertilisation-weather.py)
writes this file, `manifest.json` and the three SVG figures. It reads only the database snapshot built on
2026-10-23 (`db/events.parquet`, `db/sites.parquet`, `db/observations.parquet`, `db/variables.parquet`) and
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` of the same build. The table gives the SHA-256 and the row count of each file (the paths are
relative to the `--db` and `--calculated` folders); the script stops when a hash does not agree. The folder
[`analysis/fertilisation-weather/`](https://github.com/RJuho/inar-agri/tree/main/analysis/fertilisation-weather) has the
lock file, the unit tests and a README. The release asset of the snapshot is not yet published.
