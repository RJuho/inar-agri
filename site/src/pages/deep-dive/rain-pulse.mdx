## Short answer

At <S site="maktau" /> (maize, Kenya), the night respiration (Reco) rose on the night after a rain day of 5 mm or more.
On day 0, Reco was <Num k="summary.maktau.reco.day0" /> ± <Num k="summary.maktau.reco.day0Se" /> times the pre-rain level
(ratio to the temperature model; mean ± SE, n = <Num k="summary.maktau.reco.day0N" digits={0} /> events). In the events with no
rain day up to day 12, Reco on day 12 was <Num k={L('reco', 12, 'mean')} /> ± <Num k={L('reco', 12, 'se')} /> (n = <Num k={L('reco', 12, 'n')} digits={0} />).
At the Finnish fields, only <Num k={recoUsed.min} digits={0} /> to <Num k={recoUsed.max} digits={0} />
events per site had enough measured nights, and the composite stops by day <Num k={recoLast.max} digits={0} />. At the Finnish fields, GPP
on the rain day minus the pre-pulse mean was <Num k={gpp0.min} /> to <Num k={gpp0.max} /> {U} (n = <Num k={nk(gpp0.min)} digits={0} /> to <Num k={nk(gpp0.max)} digits={0} />).
This may be an effect of the lower light on that day. At <S site="maktau" />, the NEE change on day 0 was small
(<Num k="summary.maktau.nee.day0" sign /> ± <Num k="summary.maktau.nee.day0Se" /> {U}, n = <Num k="summary.maktau.nee.day0N" digits={0} />),
but the NEE pulse was larger when the soil was drier (ρ = <Num k="swc.nee.maktau.rho" />, n = <Num k="swc.nee.maktau.n" digits={0} />).

## Data and method

The script reads the harmonised database snapshot built on {d.dataVersion.snapshot.builtAt} and the half-hour fluxes of
the calculated products <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. The rules:

1. **Rows.** The measured flag of NEE and the night flag come from the calculated products. The fits and the night values use only season nights.
2. **Season.** Finnish sites: local days from 1 May to 30 September. Maktau: all year. Fluxes at a soil temperature of 0 °C or
   less are not used.
3. **Rain.** Daily rain is the sum of the local day. A day with fewer than
   <Num k="method.minPrecipHalfHours" digits={0} /> rain half hours has no rain value. Haltiala uses the Viikki rain.
4. **Temperature model.** A Lloyd–Taylor model of the measured night NEE against the soil temperature, per site-year
   <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} note="section 4" />. The night sample has u\* ≥ <Num k="method.ustarMin" /> m s⁻¹ at Qvidja and Maktau; rows without a u\* value stay
   <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} note="section 5" />. The own fit of this dive needs <Num k="method.lloydTaylor.minN" digits={0} />
   or more nights and a range of <Num k="method.lloydTaylor.minRange" digits={0} /> K or more. It is fitted again without
   the <Num k="method.lloydTaylor.trimPercent" digits={0} /> % largest and smallest residuals. E0 is valid when E0 ± SE is
   inside 30 to 550 K <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} note="section 4" />. When the own E0 is not valid, the site E0 is used. When that is not valid, the pooled E0 of all valid site-years is used. Rref is always
   fitted per site-year. A site-year without a night sample has no model.
5. **Night of a day.** For Reco, the night of day d runs from the evening of day d to the next morning. NEE and GPP
   use the calendar day.
6. **Daily values.** Reco = mean measured night NEE / mean model value, with
   <Num k="method.minNightHalfHours" digits={0} /> or more night half hours. GPP = mean GPP of the measured day half hours
   (<Num k="method.minDayHalfHours" digits={0} /> or more). NEE = f × night mean + (1 − f) × day mean, where f is the share of
   the half hours of the day with a night flag (both minimums; the u\* rule applies to the night mean). Light = mean PPFD
   (SW_IN at Qvidja and Maktau) on the measured day half hours.
7. **Events.** Day 0 is a season day with rain at or above the threshold (2, 5 or 10 mm). Days −3 to −1 have a rain value
   below it (they need rain data, not the season). An event stops at day 15, the next day with rain at or above the
   threshold, a day without rain data or the season end.
8. **Management.** An event is dropped when days −3 to +15 touch a harvest, cut, grazing, tillage or sowing of the site
   <Cite ids={['haltiala-management', 'qvidja-management', 'viikki-management']} />. Maktau has events only for 2019 and 2020.
9. **Haltiala check.** A Haltiala event counts only when the soil water content (SWC) on day 0 or 1 is
   <Num k="method.swcRise" /> m³ m⁻³ or more above day −1.
10. **Pre-pulse.** The pre-pulse mean is the mean of days −3 to −1. It needs <Num k="method.minPreDays" digits={0} /> of
    the 3 days. Reco is divided by it (pre-pulse = 1). NEE and GPP are a difference to it (pre-pulse = 0).
11. **Composite.** Mean, SD, SE and n per site, threshold and day. A day with n < <Num k="method.minCompositeN" digits={0} />
    is not drawn. The pulse of an event is the mean of days 0 to 2 (<Num k="method.minPulseDays" digits={0} /> or more of the 3 days).
12. **SWC anomaly.** SWC on day −1 minus the site median SWC of the season days. For the 5 mm events: OLS slope, Pearson
    r and Spearman ρ with p, per site with <Num k="method.minRelationN" digits={0} /> or more events.

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Few usable events in Finland.** An event needs measured nights on 2 of the 3 days before the rain, and a night needs 4 measured half hours after the u* rule. Before the rain, the measured fraction of the half hours was <Num k={fracPre.min} /> (<S site={fracPre.minSite} />) to
  <Num k={fracPre.max} /> (<S site={fracPre.maxSite} />). The day-0 fraction divided by the pre-pulse fraction was
  <Num k={fracRatio.min} /> to <Num k={fracRatio.max} />, so the rain day itself did not clearly reduce the data. The short summer nights of the Finnish sites may also give few night half hours.
- **Light on day 0.** The day-0 light was <Num k={light0.min} /> ± <Num k={light0.min.replace(/lightRatioDay0$/, 'lightRatioSd')} /> (SD,
  <S site={light0.minSite} />, n = <Num k={light0.min.replace(/lightRatioDay0$/, 'lightRatioN')} digits={0} />) to
  <Num k={light0.max} /> ± <Num k={light0.max.replace(/lightRatioDay0$/, 'lightRatioSd')} /> (<S site={light0.maxSite} />,
  n = <Num k={light0.max.replace(/lightRatioDay0$/, 'lightRatioN')} digits={0} />) of the pre-pulse light at the Finnish sites. The lower GPP on day 0 may follow from the lower light. The dive has no cloud data and does not test this.
- **Temperature model at Maktau.** The Maktau site fit was not valid: its E0 stopped at the lower bound. The soil
  moisture may confound the temperature fit. The Maktau years without a valid own fit use the pooled E0 of <Num k="pooledE0" digits={0} /> K. Of all site-years,
  <Num k="fitMethodCounts.own" digits={0} /> use an own fit, <Num k="fitMethodCounts.site" digits={0} /> the site fit
  and <Num k="fitMethodCounts.pooled" digits={0} /> the pooled E0. <Num k="fitMethodCounts.none" digits={0} /> Maktau site-years have no night sample and no model (2014 to 2017 have no fluxes; 2023 has the fill rows of MKT-01).
- **Haltiala rain.** The rain is from Viikki. The SWC check dropped <Num k="events.haltiala.t5.droppedSwcCheck" digits={0} />
  of <Num k="events.haltiala.t5.found" digits={0} /> 5 mm events. It assumes that a SWC rise shows rain at Haltiala.
- **Long lags.** Only events without rain reach day 10. Late days therefore show dry spells. At
  <S site="maktau" />, n fell from <Num k="summary.maktau.reco.day0N" digits={0} /> on day 0 to
  <Num k={L('reco', 10, 'n')} digits={0} /> on day 10.
- **Management.** The management rule dropped <Num k="events.qvidja.t5.droppedManagement" digits={0} /> of
  <Num k="events.qvidja.t5.found" digits={0} /> 5 mm events at <S site="qvidja" />. The database has Maktau management
  only for 2019 and 2020. Field work after 2020 can therefore be inside Maktau events.
- **Open findings** <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-ANT-01`}>ANT-01</a>, <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-SAR-01`}>SAR-01</a> and
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>VII-01</a> (status: waits for provider): the local days of Anttila,
  Särkisuo, Viikki and the Haltiala rain hold the true local hours 02:00 to 02:00, because the values sit 2 hours early. The night flag is from radiation and is not affected.
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-08`}>QVI-08</a> (status: waits for provider): events stop on days without rain data, so the Qvidja rain gap of 2022 gives no events.
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-09`}>QVI-09</a> (status: known loss, documented): the lost
  meteorology is outside the season. <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-16`}>QVI-16</a> (status: open): the frozen-soil
  SWC is outside the season. <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-01`}>MKT-01</a> (status: resolved, handled in the build):
  events stop at the Maktau fill rows.
  <a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-MKT-03`}>MKT-03</a> (status: new, verified, ask provider): the Maktau 2024 events may hold repeated NEE values.
- **Snapshot.** The snapshot is not yet published as a release. The hashes in "Reproduce" identify it.
