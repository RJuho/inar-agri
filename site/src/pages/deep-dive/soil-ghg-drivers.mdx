## Short answer

The drained peat site <E site="sarkisuo" />Särkisuo had a much larger CH₄ flux than the mineral site
<E site="anttila" />Anttila on the same days: the mean daily difference was <Num k="soilTypeCompare.ch4.diffMgPerDay.mean" digits={0} unit="mg CH₄ m⁻² d⁻¹" />
(95 % CI <Num k="soilTypeCompare.ch4.diffMgPerDay.lo" digits={0} /> to <Num k="soilTypeCompare.ch4.diffMgPerDay.hi" digits={0} />,
n = <ND k="soilTypeCompare.ch4.nDays" /> days). At Särkisuo, the CH₄ flux rose with soil temperature (Spearman ρ = <RT g="ch4" s="sarkisuo" />,
Q₁₀ = <Q g="ch4" s="sarkisuo" />). At Anttila, CH₄ was near zero and had no clear relation to soil temperature or soil
water. The N₂O flux rose with soil temperature at Särkisuo (ρ = <RT g="n2o" s="sarkisuo" />). At Anttila the relation was
weak (ρ = <RT g="n2o" s="anttila" />), and at <E site="viikki" />Viikki there was no clear relation (ρ = <RT g="n2o" s="viikki" />).
Soil water content gave only weak relations (|ρ| ≤ <Num k="water.n2o.sarkisuo.spearman.rho" abs />). The <ND k="method.fertDays" /> days after a
fertilisation held a large part of the annual N₂O sum at Särkisuo (<SH s="sarkisuo" y="2022" /> in 2022, a partial year, and
<SH s="sarkisuo" y="2023" /> in 2023). These days are only <Num k="sensitivity.shareInWindows.sarkisuo.2023.shareOfDays" percent digits={0} /> of
the days of 2023 and <Num k="sensitivity.shareInWindows.sarkisuo.2022.shareOfDays" percent digits={0} /> of the days with data in 2022.
All fluxes are gap-filled values, and the results are correlations, not causes.

## Data and method

**Snapshot and script.** The script `analysis/soil-ghg-drivers/soil-ghg-drivers.py` reads the harmonised database
snapshot built on {d.dataVersion.snapshot.builtAt}: `db/observations.parquet` (columns `site_id`, `ts_start`, `ch4`,
`n2o`, `tsoil`, `swc`), `db/sites.parquet`, `db/variables.parquet` (unit check) and `db/events.parquet` (fertilisation
dates). It checks the SHA-256 and the row count of each file before it reads data (section "Reproduce").

**Sites, gases and units.** CH₄ is only at <E site="anttila" />Anttila and <E site="sarkisuo" />Särkisuo (2022 and 2023).
N₂O is at Anttila, Särkisuo (2022 and 2023) and <E site="viikki" />Viikki (2022 to 2025). The fluxes are the gap-filled
values of the data providers; the database has no measured CH₄ or N₂O values. The units are the units of the database:
CH₄ in {UCH4}, N₂O in {UN2O}. A positive flux is an emission to the atmosphere. The soil drivers are `tsoil` (°C) and `swc`
({SWC}) of the shallowest sensor. At Anttila and Särkisuo the depth is probably <ND k="sites.anttila.sensorDepthCm" /> cm:
Shurpali et al. give <ND k="sites.anttila.sensorDepthCm" /> cm <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 4, Sect. 3.1" />, but the
database lists the depth of `TS_1_1_1` and `SWC_1_1_1` as not known. At Viikki the depth is
<ND k="sites.viikki.sensorDepthCm" /> cm (soil pit layer 1, `docs/harmonised-database.md`).

**Soil type.** The soil type is one fixed value per site. Anttila has a mineral soil (silt loam). Särkisuo has a drained
organic soil, mainly sedge peat <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 2, Sect. 2.1; p. 3, Table 2" />. Shurpali et al. call the
fields adjacent grasslands with the same climate <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 2" />. The soil organic carbon at 0 to 10 cm is
<Num k="literatureByKey.anttilaSocPct.value" /> % at Anttila and <Num k="literatureByKey.sarkisuoSocPct.value" digits={1} /> % at
Särkisuo <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 2, Table 1" />. Viikki (field Alaniitty 1) has a clay loam soil
<Cite ids={['vettikkat2026']} note="p. 2, Sect. 2.1; p. 3" />; Gao et al. give a clay texture and artificially drained
Stagnosols and Planosols <Cite ids={['gao2026']} note="p. 3" />. Thus the dive has one peat site and two mineral sites.

**1. Daily values.** A local day is the calendar day of `ts_start` + `utc_offset_min` (UTC+2). The script takes the
daily mean of each variable. A daily mean is kept when the variable has <ND k="method.minValuesPerDay" /> or more of the
48 half hours of the day; else the daily value is missing. Each analysis uses the days on which all its variables have a
daily value. For the text only, daily CH₄ is also given in mg CH₄ m⁻² d⁻¹ and daily N₂O in mg N₂O m⁻² d⁻¹ (molar mass
<Num k="method.molarMass.ch4" digits={3} /> and <Num k="method.molarMass.n2o" digits={3} /> g mol⁻¹).

**2. Temperature response (Figure 1).** Per site and gas, the daily `tsoil` goes into bins of
<Num k="method.tBin" digits={0} /> °C (bin number = floor(`tsoil` / width), bin centre = (floor(x / width) + 0.5) × width).
A bin needs <ND k="method.minBinDays" /> or more days; the other bins are not used.
Per bin, the script gives the median and the 25 % and 75 % quantiles of the daily flux. The Spearman rank correlation ρ
uses all days. The script fits F = F₁₀ × Q₁₀^((T − 10) / 10) to the bin medians that are above zero, at the bin centres.
The fit is in log space, ln F = ln F₁₀ + k (T − 10), and Q₁₀ = exp(10 k). Thus each bin has the same relative weight.
The fit needs <ND k="method.fitMinBins" /> or more bins with a median above zero, and these bins must be
<Num k="method.fitMinPositiveShare" percent digits={0} /> or more of all bins (all bins = the bins with
<ND k="method.minBinDays" /> or more days); else there is no fit. The 95 % CI of Q₁₀ is
exp(10 (k ± t × SE(k))), with the t quantile for n − 2 degrees of freedom (n = number of bins). R² is in log space.

**3. Water response (Figure 2).** The same, with `swc` in bins of <Num k="method.wBin" /> {SWC} (bin = floor of
`swc` / <Num k="method.wBin" />, same centre rule), and the Spearman ρ. There is no fit.

**4. Joint effect.** Per site and gas, an ordinary least squares (OLS) regression of the daily flux on z(`tsoil`),
z(`swc`) and their product, with a constant. The script uses the days that have a daily flux, `tsoil` and `swc` (the
OLS days). z = (x − mean) / SD, with the mean and SD (n − 1) of the OLS days of that site and gas. A coefficient of
`tsoil` or `swc` is the change of the flux for 1 SD of that driver when the other driver is at its mean. The standard
errors are Newey–West (HAC) with <ND k="method.hacLags" /> lags, Bartlett kernel and no small-sample correction
(statsmodels). The 95 % CI and the p-values use the normal distribution. The script gives the coefficients with the
95 % CI, the p-values and R².

**5. Soil type (Figure 4).** (a) Annual sums per site, local calendar year and gas: the sum of the 30-min values × 1800 s,
in {KCH4} and {KN2O}, with the coverage (values / half hours of the year). The conversion is: sum of the 30-min fluxes ×
1800 s gives µmol CH₄ m⁻² or nmol N₂O m⁻²; × molar mass (<Num k="method.molarMass.ch4" digits={3} /> g mol⁻¹ for CH₄,
<Num k="method.molarMass.n2o" digits={3} /> g mol⁻¹ for N₂O) gives g m⁻²; × 10 gives kg ha⁻¹. A year with a coverage below
<Num k="method.partialCoverage" percent digits={0} /> is a partial year. The script compares the 2022 sums with Table 3 of
Shurpali et al. 2025 <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 6, Table 3" />. Viikki gets annual N₂O sums too. (b) Paired
days: the local days of 2022 and 2023 on which both Särkisuo and Anttila have a daily value of the gas. The script gives
the daily distributions (median, 25 % and 75 %) and the mean daily difference Särkisuo − Anttila. The 95 % CI of the
difference comes from a block bootstrap. The <ND k="soilTypeCompare.ch4.diff.nDays" /> paired days, in date order, are
cut into blocks of <ND k="method.blockDays" /> days. The last block is shorter
(<ND k="soilTypeCompare.ch4.diff.lastBlockDays" /> days), and the script keeps it. Each replicate draws as many blocks as
there are (<ND k="soilTypeCompare.ch4.diff.nBlocks" />), with replacement. The statistic of a replicate is the mean of all
days in the drawn blocks. The script uses `numpy.random.default_rng` with seed {raw.method.bootSeed} and
<ND k="method.bootReps" /> replicates, and gives the 2.5 % and 97.5 % percentiles as the 95 % CI. N₂O uses the same days and blocks.

**6. Fertilisation days (N₂O).** Steps 2 to 4 again for N₂O, without the <ND k="method.fertDays" /> local days that
start on each fertilisation event of `db/events.parquet` (rows with `type` = `fertilisation`; the event day is day 0).
"Days removed" is the number of days with a daily N₂O value in these windows, all years of the site together.
The script also gives the share of the N₂O sum of each site-year that is on these days: the sum of the daily N₂O means
of the days in the windows, divided by the sum of all daily N₂O means of the year. "Fert. days" is the number of local
days of the calendar year in the windows (windows that overlap count one time). The events are: Anttila
{fertDates(d, 'anttila')}; Särkisuo {fertDates(d, 'sarkisuo')}; Viikki {fertDates(d, 'viikki')}.

**7. Heat map (Figure 3).** The mean daily flux per cell of <Num k="method.mapTBin" digits={0} /> °C `tsoil` ×
<Num k="method.mapWBin" /> {SWC} `swc` (cell = floor(`tsoil` / <Num k="method.mapTBin" digits={0} />) and
floor(`swc` / <Num k="method.mapWBin" />)). A cell needs <ND k="method.minCellDays" /> or more days.

The script logs each step that removes rows or days; the counts are in `results.json` (`dataVersion.steps`). It also
prints the open findings of the Data Report that touch this dive (section "Limits and uncertainty").

## Results

### Soil temperature

<Figure id="figure-1" svgFile="analysis/soil-ghg-drivers/figures/1-temperature-response.svg" view={VIEW1} />

**CH₄.** At <E site="sarkisuo" />Särkisuo, the daily CH₄ flux had a strong positive rank correlation with soil
temperature (ρ = <RT g="ch4" s="sarkisuo" />, n = <ND k="temperature.ch4.sarkisuo.n" /> days). The Q₁₀ fit gave
Q₁₀ = <Q g="ch4" s="sarkisuo" /> and F₁₀ = <Num k="temperature.ch4.sarkisuo.fit.f10" digits={3} unit={UCH4} />
(R² in log space <Num k="temperature.ch4.sarkisuo.fit.r2Log" />, <ND k="temperature.ch4.sarkisuo.fit.nBins" /> bins).
At <E site="anttila" />Anttila, the CH₄ flux had no clear relation to soil temperature (ρ = <RT g="ch4" s="anttila" />,
p = <Num k="temperature.ch4.anttila.spearman.p" />, n = <ND k="temperature.ch4.anttila.n" /> days). The median daily

<ND k="temperature.ch4.anttila.fit.nBins" /> of the <ND k="temperature.ch4.anttila.fit.nBinsAll" /> bins had a median

**N₂O.** At Särkisuo, the N₂O flux rose with soil temperature (ρ = <RT g="n2o" s="sarkisuo" />, n =

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Gap-filled fluxes only.** The database has only the gap-filled CH₄ and N₂O values. In 2022, the share of measured
  30-min values after the quality filter, as a share of the whole year, was {MC.anttila.ch4} % (CH₄) and {MC.anttila.n2o} % (N₂O)
  at Anttila, and {MC.sarkisuo.ch4} % and {MC.sarkisuo.n2o} % at Särkisuo <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 3, Sect. 2.3.2" />.
  At Särkisuo the CH₄ and N₂O measurements started on 6 May 2022 <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 3" />. No measured
  share is known for 2023. Shurpali et
  al. filled the gaps with three machine-learning models (random forest, XGB and kNN) and used the median of the three
  <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 3" />. The main text does not list the model inputs. If soil temperature or soil
  water is an input, a part of each relation on this page comes from the model, not from the measurements. The gap-filling
  method of the Viikki N₂O is not in the files of this project. The size of this effect is not known.
- **No uncertainty of the annual sums.** The database has only the ensemble median of the gap-filled fluxes. Thus the
  annual sums of this dive have no uncertainty range. Shurpali et al. give the range of the three algorithms, for 2022
  only <Cite ids={['shurpali2025']} note="p. 6, Table 3" />.
- **No wind.** The Anttila and Särkisuo files have no wind speed and no u*. The dive therefore does not study wind.
- **Two soil drivers only.** The dive uses `tsoil` and `swc` of one depth per site. The raw files also have the water
  table depth (Anttila 2022, Viikki) and the soil O₂ (Anttila 2022), but these are not in the harmonised database. The
  soil type is one value per site; the dive has one peat site, so it cannot separate the soil type from the other
  differences of the two fields. The Viikki sensors are at <ND k="sites.viikki.sensorDepthCm" /> cm, not at
  <ND k="sites.anttila.sensorDepthCm" /> cm. Thus the Viikki relations are not directly comparable with those of Anttila
  and Särkisuo.
- **Viikki N₂O unit.** The unit of the Viikki N₂O can be µg N₂O or µg N₂O-N (finding <FINDING id="VII-06" />; status:
  waits for provider) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />. The build assumes µg N₂O. If it is N₂O-N, all Viikki N₂O values
  of the database are too small: the true values are <Num k="method.n2oNFactor" /> times larger, for example the 2022 sum would be
  <Num k="annual.viikki.2022.n2o.kgHaIfN2oN" digits={1} unit={KN2O} />. The rank correlations do not change; the Viikki
  sums, medians, coefficients and heat map values change by this factor.
- **Time stamps.** The raw stamps of Anttila, Särkisuo and Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as UTC+2 (findings
  <FINDING id="ANT-01" />, <FINDING id="SAR-01" /> and <FINDING id="VII-01" />; status: waits for provider)
  <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />. A local day of these sites therefore holds the hours from 02:00 to 02:00 of the true
  local time. The changes of the daily means are probably small. The annual sums change only by the hours at the ends of
  the year. The script does not give the size.
- **Särkisuo 2022 is a partial year.** The Särkisuo CH₄ and N₂O values start on 2022-05-06 (finding <FINDING id="SAR-07" />;
  status: resolved) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />. The 2022 sums have a coverage of
  <Num k="annual.sarkisuo.2022.ch4.coverage" percent digits={0} />, and the paired days start on that day.
- **Zero curtain.** A zero curtain is a period in which the soil temperature stays near 0 °C while the soil water
  freezes or thaws. In winter, `tsoil` stays near 0 °C for weeks. At Särkisuo it is constant for most of the winter
  2022–23; a sensor floor is an open question (findings <FINDING id="SAR-04" />, status: waits for provider;
  <FINDING id="ANT-05" /> and <FINDING id="VII-03" />, status: resolved) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />. The lowest
  temperature bins therefore hold many winter days with different fluxes, for example
  <ND k="temperature.ch4.anttila.bins.0.n" /> days in the first bin at Anttila.
- **Viikki soil temperature 2025.** The Viikki `tsoil` sensor at <ND k="sites.viikki.sensorDepthCm" /> cm has spikes up
  to <Num k="method.findingNumbers.VII-04.maxTsoil" digits={1} unit="°C" /> after field work in May, August and September 2025
  (finding <FINDING id="VII-04" />; status: waits for provider) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />. On
  <ND k="method.findingNumbers.VII-04.days" /> days the daily mean can be <Num k="method.findingNumbers.VII-04.dailyBiasLo" digits={0} /> to
  <Num k="method.findingNumbers.VII-04.dailyBiasHi" digits={0} unit="°C" /> too high. Some of these days are
  in the <ND k="method.fertDays" /> days after the fertilisation of 2025-05-10. The dive does not remove them.
- **Autocorrelated days.** Daily values next to each other are not independent. The p-values of the Spearman ρ assume
  independent days and are therefore too small. The OLS uses HAC standard errors, and the paired difference uses a block
  bootstrap, but these corrections are approximate.
- **Fertilisation days.** The <ND k="method.fertDays" /> days after a fertilisation hold a large share of the N₂O sum at some site-years (up to
  <SH s="sarkisuo" y="2022" /> at Särkisuo 2022; this partial year has no values before {raw.soilTypeCompare.ch4.first}, so its
  share is of a smaller sum). The N₂O relations change without these days (section "Fertilisation days").
  The event dates come from the management records; an event that is not in `db/events.parquet` is not removed.
- **Correlation, not cause.** Soil temperature, soil water, plant growth and management change together during the
  year. The relations on this page are correlations. They do not show which variable causes a flux.
- **Snapshot.** The database snapshot of this dive (built {d.dataVersion.snapshot.builtAt}) is not yet published as a
  release asset. The hashes in "Reproduce" identify it.
