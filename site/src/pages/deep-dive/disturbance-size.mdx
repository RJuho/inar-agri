## Short answer

This dive has an iteration: <a href={`${base}deep-dive/disturbance-detail/`}>Deep Dive #4, *Management disturbances in detail*</a>
compares cleaning cuts, silage harvests and grain harvests per site, searches the day of the flux drop around each recorded
harvest date, and compares the fertilisation episodes with control windows of the same season and weather.

We compared the mean daily flux in the <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days after a management episode with
the mean in the <Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} /> days before it. A higher NEE means less net uptake of CO₂.
The size of a disturbance is this change, and also the *excess*: the sum of the daily differences to the level
before the episode over <Num k="method.excessDays" digits={0} /> days.

- **Harvest or cut** (n = <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nFlux" digits={0} />): NEE <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nee.delta.mean" sign unit={U} />
  (<Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % interval <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nee.delta.ciLo" sign /> to
  <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nee.delta.ciHi" sign />). NEE increased in <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nee.delta.nPositive" digits={0} /> of
  the <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nFlux" digits={0} /> episodes. GPP fell by <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.gpp.delta.mean" abs unit={U} />,
  that is <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.relGppPct.mean" abs digits={0} unit="%" /> of the GPP before the episode. The change of
  Reco (<Num k="kinds.harvestCut.reco.delta.mean" sign unit={U} />) has an interval that includes zero. The excess of
  NEE over <Num k="method.excessDays" digits={0} /> days was <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nee.excess.mean" sign digits={0} unit={UE} />
  (n = <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nee.excess.n" digits={0} />).
- **Grazing** (n = <Num k="kinds.grazing.nFlux" digits={0} />, Qvidja only): NEE <Num k="kinds.grazing.nee.delta.mean" sign unit={U} />,
  GPP <Num k="kinds.grazing.gpp.delta.mean" sign unit={U} />. The direction is the same as for a harvest. NEE increased in
  <Num k="kinds.grazing.nee.delta.nPositive" digits={0} /> of the <Num k="kinds.grazing.nFlux" digits={0} /> episodes. With so few
  episodes the interval is not reliable.
- **Fertilisation** (n = <Num k="kinds.fertilisation.nFlux" digits={0} />): the interval of the change of NEE includes zero
  (<Num k="kinds.fertilisation.nee.delta.mean" sign unit={U} />, <Num k="kinds.fertilisation.nee.delta.ciLo" sign /> to
  <Num k="kinds.fertilisation.nee.delta.ciHi" sign />). The mean changes of GPP and Reco are positive, but their

  <Num k="kinds.fertilisation.reco.deltaAdj.mean" sign unit={U} /> (n = <Num k="kinds.fertilisation.reco.deltaAdj.n" digits={0} />).
- **Tillage** (n = <Num k="kinds.tillage.nFlux" digits={0} />, autumn, bare soil): NEE <Num k="kinds.tillage.nee.delta.mean" sign unit={U} />.
  The interval includes zero (n = <Num k="kinds.tillage.nFlux" digits={0} />, <Num k="kinds.tillage.nee.delta.ciLo" sign /> to
  <Num k="kinds.tillage.nee.delta.ciHi" sign />). **Tillage with fertilisation** (mostly spring field work, n = <Num k="kinds.tillageFert.nFlux" digits={0} />):
  NEE <Num k="kinds.tillageFert.nee.delta.mean" sign unit={U} /> (<Num k="kinds.tillageFert.nee.delta.ciLo" sign /> to

## Data and method

**Events.** We used the dated events of Haltiala, Qvidja and Viikki, from their management files
<Cite ids={['qvidja-management', 'viikki-management', 'haltiala-management']} /> (one Viikki sowing comes from an
article, see the Events page). The build stores them in `db/events.parquet`: <Num k="counts.events" digits={0} /> events
of these three sites. The events of Anttila, Särkisuo and Maktau are not analysed. We analysed four classes:
*harvest or cut* (<Num k="counts.perType.harvest" digits={0} /> harvests and <Num k="counts.perType.cut" digits={0} /> cuts),
*grazing* (<Num k="counts.perType.grazing" digits={0} />, all at Qvidja), *fertilisation* (<Num k="counts.perType.fertilisation" digits={0} />)
and *tillage* (<Num k="counts.perType.tillage" digits={0} />). Sowing (<Num k="counts.perType.sowing" digits={0} />),
spraying (<Num k="counts.perType.spraying" digits={0} />) and other events are not analysed. They are listed with the
episode when they are near. A fertilisation is *mineral* when the product text names a mineral product (the word mineral,
or the product names saltpetre, salpietari, AXAN, Typpi or CAN; the NPK product of Qvidja has the word mineral in its text). All other products (manure, compost, molasses, by-products) are *organic*.

**Episodes.** We sorted the events of each site by date. An event joins the current episode when it starts not more
than <Num k="method.mergeDays" digits={0} /> days after the end of that episode, whatever its class. The end of an
episode is the last day of its events (`date_end` where the file gives it, for example the grazing periods). The class
set of the episode gives its *kind*: harvest or cut; grazing; fertilisation; tillage; tillage with fertilisation
(spring field work: tillage, sowing and fertilisation within a few days); harvest or cut with fertilisation; or other.
*Other* is any other combination of classes. The current events give no such episode. Sowing, spraying and other events do not make an
episode and do not join one. They are listed as events near the episode.
This gives <Num k="counts.episodes" digits={0} /> episodes from <Num k="counts.classEvents" digits={0} /> events of the four classes.

**Daily values.** NEE, GPP and Reco are the daily sums of the gap-filled, partitioned fluxes in g C m⁻² d⁻¹ from
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. NEE &gt; 0 is a net release of CO₂. GPP is the
uptake by photosynthesis. Reco is the temperature model of the night-time partitioning, and GPP is Reco − NEE. The
soil water content (`swc`, m³ m⁻³), the soil temperature and the rain come from `db/observations.parquet` as the mean
or sum per local day. A daily mean of soil water or soil temperature needs <Num k="method.minSwcRows" digits={0} /> of the
<Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} /> half hours of the day. A daily rain sum needs <Num k="method.minRainRows" digits={0} /> half hours. Haltiala has no rain sensor. For Haltiala we
used the rain of Viikki on the same day (the sites are about five kilometres apart). We did not use the soil water of
frozen days (daily mean soil temperature at or below zero: <Num k="counts.frozenSwcPct" digits={0} unit="%" /> of the days,
mostly in winter) and of Haltiala in <Num k="method.haltialaSwcSkipYear" digits={0} />
(<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-HAL-03`}>finding HAL-03</a>). A day without a soil temperature mean keeps
its soil water value. The sensor depth is <Num k="method.sensorDepthCm.haltiala" digits={0} unit="cm" /> at Haltiala
and Qvidja and <Num k="method.sensorDepthCm.viikki" digits={0} unit="cm" /> at Viikki. At Viikki the 2022 value comes
from the EC file (water-filled pore space times porosity) and from 2023 from the soil pit file. Both are the same
sensor <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.

**Windows.** For each episode we calculated the mean of each variable in these windows:

- *before*: the <Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} /> days before the first day of the episode,
- *after*: the <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} /> days after the last day of the episode (main result),
- *late*: days <Num k="method.lateFrom" digits={0} /> to <Num k="method.lateTo" digits={0} /> after the last day.

The days of the episode are not in a window. The late window needs the same minimum of days as rule 2 below, and no
other episode may start in it. Else the late value is empty. The *change* is the mean after minus the mean before. The *excess* is the
sum of (daily value − mean before) over the <Num k="method.excessDays" digits={0} /> days after the episode, in g C m⁻².
It needs <Num k="method.minExcessDays" digits={0} /> days with a value and no other episode in the
<Num k="method.excessDays" digits={0} /> days.

**Relative change.** Figure 3 gives the change in percent of a reference, for after windows of
<Num k="method.relativeAfterDays.0" digits={0} />, <Num k="method.relativeAfterDays.1" digits={0} /> and
<Num k="method.relativeAfterDays.2" digits={0} /> days after the last day of the episode. For GPP and Reco the
reference is the mean of the same flux in the <Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} /> days before the episode. For
NEE the reference is the mean GPP of the <Num k="method.gppRefDays" digits={0} /> days before the first day of the
episode, because the NEE before an event can be near zero or negative. Each reference must be at least
<Num k="method.minRefFlux" digits={1} unit={U} />. Each flux counts its own days. A day counts for NEE when NEE has a
value on that day, and the same for GPP and Reco. The three fluxes do not need the same days. The GPP reference needs
only GPP: at least <Num k="method.minGppRefDays" digits={0} /> days with a GPP value. The
<Num k="method.beforeDays" digits={0} />-day window before needs <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with a value.
An after window runs from the first day to the last day after the episode, both included. An after window of n days
needs the same share of days with a value (n × <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> /
<Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} />, rounded up). When another episode of the site starts on any day of the window,
the value is empty. The *relative change of GPP* in the table is the value of the <Num k="method.relativeAfterDays.1" digits={0} />-day
window.

**Seasonal baseline.** The windows also contain the change of the season. For each episode we took the same calendar
windows in the other years of the same site. A shifted period goes from the start of the before window to the end of
the after window. We used a year only when no episode of the site (from its first to its last day) overlaps this
shifted period. The other years are the years with
data of the site. The shifted windows need the same minimum of days as rule 2. The baseline is
the mean of these seasonal changes, when at least <Num k="method.minBaselineYears" digits={0} /> years are available.
The *adjusted change* is the change minus the baseline. <Num k="counts.withBaseline" digits={0} /> of the
<Num k="counts.fluxUsed" digits={0} /> used episodes have a baseline. The Qvidja harvests in June have none: every year
has a harvest at that time.

**Rules.** The flux result uses an episode when it passes three rules:

- **Rule 1.** No other episode of the site starts in the *before* or the *after* window.
- **Rule 2.** Both windows have at least <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> days with NEE, GPP and Reco.
- **Rule 3.** In both windows, the mean measured fraction of NEE is at least <Num k="method.minFracPct" digits={0} /> %
  (`frac_measured` of the daily file).

<Num k="counts.fluxUsed" digits={0} /> episodes passed (<Num k="counts.fluxExclusions.overlap" digits={0} /> did not pass
rule 1, <Num k="counts.fluxExclusions.too-few-days" digits={0} /> rule 2, <Num k="counts.fluxExclusions.low-measured-fraction" digits={0} />
rule 3). The soil water result uses rules 1 and 2 with the soil water days: <Num k="counts.swcUsed" digits={0} /> episodes.
An episode is *rain-balanced* when the rain sums of the two windows differ by not more than
<Num k="method.rainBalanceMm" digits={0} /> mm. The rain sum of a window is the sum of the days with a valid rain sum.
Each window needs at least <Num k="method.minDays" digits={0} /> such days. <Num k="counts.swcRainBalanced" digits={0} /> episodes
are rain-balanced. We also fitted
the change of soil water against the rain difference (least squares) per kind. The intercept of this fit is the change
at equal rain.

**Statistics.** Per kind, and per kind and site: n, mean, SD, the number of episodes with a positive change, and a
<Num k="method.ciLevelPct" digits={0} /> % percentile bootstrap interval of the mean (<Num k="method.bootstrapResamples" digits={0} />
resamples, fixed seed). An interval needs at least three episodes, and with fewer than about ten episodes it is not
reliable. The tables give the single episodes.

**Checks.** (a) The same change with <Num k="method.shortDays" digits={0} />-day windows. (b) The flux result with the
episodes that did not pass rule 3. (c) The change of the *measured* day-time NEE (µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, half hours without
gap filling, at least <Num k="method.minMeasuredDay" digits={0} /> half hours per window).

<Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="p. 3473; Table C1, PDF p. 13" />. For the same event, our <Num k="method.afterDays" digits={0} />-day

mineral fertilisation. Our mineral episodes are in May, and the seasonal increase of Reco can be part of the change. Our

## Limits and uncertainty

- **Small n.** Grazing (n = <Num k="kinds.grazing.nFlux" digits={0} />), tillage (n = <Num k="kinds.tillage.nFlux" digits={0} />)
  and the site groups have few episodes. Their intervals are not reliable. The SD of the change of NEE after a harvest
  or cut is <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nee.delta.sd" unit={U} /> (n = <Num k="kinds.harvestCut.nFlux" digits={0} />).
- **Season.** The seasonal baseline removes the mean change of the other years, but only where other years are free
  of events. For the June harvests at Qvidja and the May fertilisations this is often not the case. The change is
  *associated with* the event. This analysis does not show that the event causes all of the change.
- **Mixed episodes.** An episode of spring field work has tillage, sowing and fertilisation within a few days. The
  data cannot separate their effects. <Num k="counts.withOtherEvents" digits={0} /> used episodes have other events
  from the start of the before window to the end of the late window (see the table).
- **Gap filling and partitioning.** In the used windows, the mean measured fraction of NEE is <Num k="counts.fracMeasuredMeanPct" digits={0} /> %
  (lowest <Num k="counts.fracMeasuredMinPct" digits={0} /> %). The gap-filling models do not use the event dates
  (Vira et al. use the days since the previous harvest as a predictor <Cite ids={['vira2025']} note="PDF p. 6" />;
  our providers do not). GPP and Reco are model values. The reference respiration of the Reco model comes from
  windows of <Num k="method.rrefWindowDays" digits={0} /> days, every <Num k="method.rrefStepDays" digits={0} /> days
  <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />. A short pulse of respiration can be absorbed into this reference.
- **Grazing dates.** The Qvidja management file gives grazing from 1 June 2021 (four horses, seven days) and from
  1 June 2022 (nine cows, fifteen days) <Cite ids={['qvidja-management']} />. The newer site event file of the project
  gives the same periods from 23 August 2021 and from 5 August 2022, and we used these dates. Heimsch et al. 2024
  describe grazing in August 2021 (eight cows, six days) and August 2022 (nine cows, nine days)
  <Cite ids={['heimsch2024']} note="PDF p. 2" />. The months now agree, but the number of animals in 2021 and the
  durations differ.
- **Soil water.** Haltiala `swc` is a gap-filled column at a resolution of <Num k="method.haltialaSwcResolution" digits={2} unit={US} /> with long flat periods
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-HAL-01`}>finding HAL-01</a>). Haltiala has no rain; the Viikki rain is a
  proxy. The Qvidja value is a conversion from sensor volts (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-01`}>finding QVI-01</a>);
  the level is uncertain, a change over days is less affected. At Viikki the 2022 value is derived from the EC file and the
  later values come from the soil pit file; both are the same sensor at <Num k="method.sensorDepthCm.viikki" digits={0} unit="cm" />. The soil water of frozen days is not used
  (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-QVI-16`}>finding QVI-16</a>) <Cite ids={['anomaly-report']} />.
- **Time stamps.** The raw time stamps of Viikki are UTC, but the build reads them as local time, so the Viikki values
  are <Num k="method.utcShiftHours" digits={0} /> hours early (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-01`}>finding VII-01</a>).
  On a daily scale this moves <Num k="method.utcShiftHalfHours" digits={0} /> of the <Num k="method.halfHoursPerDay" digits={0} />
  half hours of a day to the next day. The first dive tested this shift and found a small effect on the changes.
- **Soil temperature at Viikki** spikes after field work in 2025 (<a href={`${base}data-report/#finding-VII-04`}>finding VII-04</a>).
  We used the soil temperature only to find frozen days.
- **Event dates and details.** The dates come from the management files. Some differ by one or two days from the
  articles <Cite ids={['heimsch2021']} note="PDF p. 3, p. 7 and Table C1, PDF p. 13" />. The N content of some Haltiala products is not in the file.
- **GPP sign.** GPP is Reco − NEE and is not limited to positive values. When the measured NEE is above the Reco
  model, the daily GPP is negative. This occurs mainly at low light and in winter, and in the Haltiala episode of
  August 2023 after the crop was crushed <Cite ids={['calculated-doc']} />.

## Reproduce

The numbers on this page come from `site/src/deep-dives/disturbance-size.json`. The script
[`scripts/deep-dives/disturbance-size.ts`](https://github.com/RJuho/inar-agri/blob/main/scripts/deep-dives/disturbance-size.ts)
writes this file. It reads only `db/events.parquet`, `db/sites.parquet`, `db/observations.parquet`,
`data/calculated/daily.parquet` and `data/calculated/fluxes_30min.parquet`. The pure helpers are in
`scripts/lib/deep-dive/disturbance.ts` (unit test `disturbance.test.ts`).

```sh
npm run build:db && npm run calc         # make the inputs
npm run deep-dives -- disturbance-size   # write the JSON again
npm run deep-dives -- --check            # compare a fresh run with the committed JSON
```
